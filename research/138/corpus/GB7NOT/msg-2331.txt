Warning

Mercury 1.9.18 does not talk to Mercury 1.9.17 or any older version over 
the air, in either direction. A 1.9.18 station and a 1.9.x station never connect. 

Upgrade every station of a network together (on HERMES, the gateway and 
all its remote stations in the same window).

Read Upgrading to 1.9.18 before upgrading. It says what an operator sees with 
mixed versions, how to upgrade, and what was tested before this release.
