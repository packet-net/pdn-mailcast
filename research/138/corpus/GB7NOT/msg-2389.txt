1256 AM CDT FRI OCT 16 2026

...THERE IS A SLIGHT RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS FOR PARTS OF THE
MID-ATLANTIC INTO SOUTHERN NEW ENGLAND...

...SUMMARY...
THUNDERSTORMS WITH WIND-DAMAGE AND LOCALIZED BRIEF-TORNADO POTENTIAL
ARE EXPECTED ACROSS PARTS OF THE MID-ATLANTIC INTO SOUTHERN NEW
ENGLAND, MAINLY THIS AFTERNOON AND EARLY EVENING.

...MID-ATLANTIC INTO SOUTHERN NEW ENGLAND...
A DEEP, POSITIVE-TILT MID/UPPER-LEVEL TROUGH IS FORECAST TO FURTHER
AMPLIFY AS IT SPREADS OVER THE EASTERN CONUS TODAY. A SURFACE LOW
INITIALLY NEAR THE LOWER GREAT LAKES WILL MOVE QUICKLY EASTWARD
TOWARD NORTHERN NEW ENGLAND BY EVENING. A TRAILING COLD FRONT WILL
SWEEP THROUGH THE MID-ATLANTIC AND NORTHEAST FROM THIS AFTERNOON
INTO TONIGHT.

IN THE WAKE OF EARLY-DAY CLOUDS AND PRECIPITATION, SEASONABLY RICH
LOW-LEVEL MOISTURE AND MODEST DIURNAL HEATING WILL RESULT IN
SUFFICIENT DESTABILIZATION FOR STORM DEVELOPMENT ALONG THE FRONT.
VERY WEAK LAPSE RATES ABOVE 500 MB WILL CONSTRAIN MLCAPE MAGNITUDES
TO THE 500-1000 J/KG RANGE, AND RESULT IN RATHER LOW-TOPPED
CONVECTION. HOWEVER, IN AREAS WHERE TEMPERATURES CAN RISE TO
NEAR/ABOVE 70 F, RELATIVELY ROBUST BUOYANCY NEAR/BELOW 500 MB
(CHARACTERIZED BY LIFTED INDICES IN THE -4 TO -6 C RANGE) WILL
DEVELOP PRIOR TO THE FRONTAL PASSAGE. THIS BUOYANCY WILL BE
SUFFICIENT FOR DEVELOPMENT OF OCCASIONALLY ORGANIZED STORM
STRUCTURES, IN THE PRESENCE OF STRENGTHENING DEEP-LAYER FLOW/SHEAR.
35-45 KT OF FLOW IN THE 1-2 KM AGL LAYER WILL SUPPORT POTENTIAL FOR
STRONG/DAMAGING GUSTS WITH ANY SUFFICIENTLY ROBUST CONVECTION.

GENERALLY FRONT-PARALLEL FLOW MAY RESULT IN A PRIMARILY LINEAR MODE
AS CONVECTIVE COVERAGE INCREASES, THOUGH 40+ KT OF EFFECTIVE SHEAR
WILL SUPPORT POTENTIAL FOR EMBEDDED LOW-TOPPED SUPERCELLS. A
LOCALIZED WIND-DAMAGE THREAT MAY DEVELOP EARLY THIS AFTERNOON FROM
WV/WESTERN PA INTO SOUTHERN NY, WITH INITIAL FRONTAL DEVELOPMENT.
THE WIND-DAMAGE THREAT IS EXPECTED TO INCREASE THROUGH THE AFTERNOON
AS STORMS SPREAD EASTWARD INTO A SOMEWHAT WARMER AND MORE UNSTABLE
ENVIRONMENT ACROSS EASTERN PA, NJ, AND VICINITY. RATHER STRONG
LOW-LEVEL SHEAR WILL ALSO SUPPORT SOME BRIEF-TORNADO POTENTIAL WITH
QLCS CIRCULATIONS AND/OR EMBEDDED SUPERCELL STRUCTURES.

SOME WIND-DAMAGE POTENTIAL COULD SPREAD INTO SOUTHERN NEW ENGLAND BY
THIS EVENING, THOUGH MARINE-INFLUENCED LOW-LEVEL STABILITY SHOULD
EVENTUALLY CONSTRAIN THE EASTWARD EXTENT OF THE THREAT.

..DEAN/CHALMERS.. 10/16/2026

...CRITICAL FIRE WEATHER AREA FOR WESTERN L.A. COUNTY...VENTURA
COUNTY...AND SOUTHEASTERN SANTA BARBARA COUNTY IN SOUTHERN
CALIFORNIA...

...SYNOPSIS...
A MID/UPPER LOW WILL REMAIN POSITIONED JUST WEST OF THE NORTHERN
BAJA PENINSULA WITHIN A BROADER UPPER-LEVEL RIDGE OVER THE WESTERN
U.S. IN CONJUNCTION WITH A STRONG SURFACE HIGH PRESSURE SYSTEM (1044
MB PER THE 03 UTC WPC SURFACE ANALYSIS) MOVING INTO THE CENTRAL
GREAT PLAINS, THIS UPPER LOW WILL CONTINUE TO SUPPORT DRY, OFFSHORE
FLOW AND ELEVATED TO CRITICAL FIRE WEATHER CONDITIONS ACROSS
PORTIONS OF CALIFORNIA TODAY. TO THE EAST, A ROBUST UPPER-LEVEL
TROUGH WILL PIVOT EASTWARD ACROSS THE EASTERN U.S., WITH AN
ATTENDANT SURFACE LOW DEEPENING AS IT PROGRESSES NORTHEASTWARD
ACROSS THE ST. LAWRENCE RIVER VALLEY AND A STRONG COLD FRONT
ADVANCING ACROSS THE EAST/SOUTHEAST. STRONG POST-FRONTAL FLOW IN THE
WAKE OF THIS COLD FRONT WILL BRING ELEVATED FIRE WEATHER CONCERNS TO
PORTIONS OF THE LOWER MISSISSIPPI RIVER VALLEY AND ARKLATEX TODAY.

...SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA...
A CLOSED MID/UPPER LOW WILL AID IN THE MAINTENANCE OF DEEP-LAYER
EAST-NORTHEASTERLY FLOW ACROSS MUCH OF SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA, WHICH
WILL CONTINUE TO SUPPORT AN OFFSHORE WIND EVENT AND ELEVATED TO
CRITICAL FIRE WEATHER CONDITIONS TODAY. A CORRIDOR OF SUSTAINED
EAST-NORTHEASTERLY 20-30 MPH SURFACE WINDS WITH GUSTS UP TO 50-60
MPH (OCCASIONALLY HIGHER IN TERRAIN-FAVORED AND EXPOSED ELEVATED
AREAS) IS FORECAST ACROSS WESTERN L.A, VENTURA, AND SOUTHEASTERN
SANTA BARBARA COUNTIES, WITH A PEAK DURING THE LATE MORNING/EARLY
AFTERNOON HOURS BEFORE GRADUALLY DIMINISHING BEGINNING LATER THIS
AFTERNOON. WITH MINIMUM RH VALUES LIKELY DROPPING TO NEAR 15%
(LOCALLY LOWER), THIS WILL FAVOR A CRITICAL FIRE WEATHER THREAT.
ELEVATED TO LOCALLY CRITICAL CONDITIONS ARE ALSO EXPECTED FARTHER
WEST TOWARDS THE CENTRAL CA COAST WHERE RH REDUCTIONS TO 15% AND
SUSTAINED EASTERLY WINDS OF 15-20 MPH ARE EXPECTED.

...SIERRA NEVADA, SACRAMENTO VALLEY INTO THE NORTH BAY AREA...
RESIDUAL EASTERLY/NORTHEASTERLY DOWNSLOPE WINDS OF 10-15 MPH
(LOCALLY UP TO 20 MPH) ARE EXPECTED TODAY ACROSS THE WESTERN SLOPES
OF THE SIERRA NEVADA. POOR OVERNIGHT HUMIDITY RECOVERIES ARE
OBSERVED AS OF 0600 UTC, WITH RH VALUES NOTED TO RANGE FROM 15-25%
ACROSS PORTIONS OF THE WESTERN SLOPES. RH VALUES SHOULD FURTHER
DECREASE TO NEAR 10% TODAY. COUPLED WITH RECEPTIVE FUELS AND THE
BREEZY EAST-NORTHEAST WINDS, THIS WILL HELP MAINTAIN AN ELEVATED
FIRE WEATHER THREAT ACROSS THIS REGION TODAY. NORTH-NORTHEAST WINDS
OF 10-15 MPH AND MINIMUM RH VALUES OF 10-15% WILL ALSO BRING
ELEVATED FIRE WEATHER CONCERNS TO PORTIONS OF THE SACRAMENTO VALLEY,
NAPA VALLEY, AND NORTH BAY AREAS TODAY.

...LOWER MISSISSIPPI RIVER VALLEY AND ARKLATEX REGION...
A STRONG SURFACE HIGH PRESSURE SYSTEM WILL BUILD INTO THE CENTRAL
PLAINS TODAY IN THE WAKE OF THE PASSAGE OF A SUBSTANTIAL SURFACE
COLD FRONT ADVANCING ACROSS THE EAST/SOUTHEAST. THE RESULTANT
SURFACE PRESSURE GRADIENT BETWEEN THESE TWO FEATURES WILL FAVOR
SUSTAINED NORTHERLY WINDS OF 10-15 MPH (WITH OCCASIONAL GUSTS UP TO
AROUND 30-35 MPH POSSIBLE) ACROSS PORTIONS OF THE LOWER MISSISSIPPI
RIVER VALLEY AND ARKLATEX WITHIN THE DRY, POST-FRONTAL AIR MASS.
WITH RH REDUCTIONS AS LOW AS ~25% OVER RECEPTIVE FUELS (ERCS UP TO
THE 80-90TH PERCENTILES), ELEVATED FIRE WEATHER CONDITIONS ARE
EXPECTED FOR AT LEAST A FEW HOURS THIS AFTERNOON. WHILE RH WILL
IMPROVE INTO THE OVERNIGHT HOURS, GUSTY SURFACE WINDS ARE EXPECTED
TO CONTINUE INTO SATURDAY MORNING. ONLY A MINOR SOUTHWARD EXPANSION
TO THE EXISTING ELEVATED AREA WAS MADE WITH THIS UPDATE.

..CHALMERS.. 10/16/2026
