SPC 1200Z DAY 1 OUTLOOK

DAY 1 CONVECTIVE OUTLOOK
NWS STORM PREDICTION CENTER NORMAN OK
1250 AM CDT WED OCT 14 2026

VALID 141200Z - 151200Z

...THERE IS A SLIGHT RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS ACROSS PARTS OF
THE SOUTHERN PLAINS...

...SUMMARY...
THUNDERSTORMS WITH SEVERE WIND GUSTS AND POTENTIALLY A TORNADO
THREAT WILL BE POSSIBLE THIS AFTERNOON AND EVENING ACROSS PARTS OF
THE SOUTHERN PLAINS. A MARGINAL WIND-DAMAGE THREAT WILL ALSO BE
POSSIBLE FROM PARTS OF THE OZARKS INTO THE CENTRAL APPALACHIANS.

...SOUTHERN PLAINS...
AT MID-LEVELS, SOUTHWESTERLY FLOW WILL BE IN PLACE ACROSS MUCH OF
THE SOUTH-CENTRAL U.S. TODAY, AS A LARGE-SCALE TROUGH AND LOW MOVE
THROUGH THE DESERT SOUTHWEST. AT THE SURFACE, A COLD FRONT WILL
ADVANCE SOUTHWARD THIS MORNING ACROSS NORTHERN OKLAHOMA AND THE
TEXAS PANHANDLE. NUMEROUS THUNDERSTORMS WILL BE LIKELY THIS MORNING
NEAR THE FRONT. THE EFFECTIVE BOUNDARY IS EXPECTED TO BE FURTHER
SOUTH, WHICH WILL SHIFT THE EFFECTIVE DESTABILIZATION FURTHER SOUTH
AS WELL. BY EARLY AFTERNOON, AN AXIS OF MODERATE INSTABILITY IS
FORECAST TO BE LOCATED FROM WEST TEXAS NORTHEASTWARD INTO
SOUTHWESTERN AND SOUTH-CENTRAL OKLAHOMA, WHERE MLCAPE WILL LIKELY
RANGE FROM 1500 TO 2000 J/KG. AREAS THAT DESTABILIZE SIGNIFICANTLY
WILL HAVE POTENTIAL FOR SEVERE STORMS.

RAP FORECAST SOUNDINGS NEAR THE INSTABILITY AXIS BY MID TO LATE
AFTERNOON HAVE SUPERCELL WIND PROFILES, WITH GRADUALLY VEERING WINDS
WITH HEIGHT IN THE LOW-LEVELS AND ABOUT 50 KNOTS OF SOUTHWESTERLY
FLOW IN THE MID-LEVELS. THIS WILL SUPPORT SUPERCELL DEVELOPMENT WITH
STORMS THAT FORM IN THE STRONGER INSTABILITY AND REMAIN DISCRETE.
SUPERCELLS WILL BE CAPABLE OF SEVERE WIND GUSTS, HAIL AND PERHAPS A
TORNADO OR TWO. DURING THE EVENING, A TRANSITION TO LINEAR MODE IS
EXPECTED, BUT A THREAT FOR EMBEDDED SUPERCELLS WILL CONTINUE. THE
MORE INTENSE LINE SEGMENTS COULD PRODUCE SEVERE WIND GUSTS,
POTENTIALLY ABOVE 70 MPH.

...OZARKS/OHIO VALLEY/CENTRAL APPALACHIANS...
AN ANTICYCLONIC FLOW PATTERN WILL BE IN PLACE OVER THE EASTERN HALF
OF THE NATION TODAY. AT THE SURFACE, A COLD FRONT WILL ADVANCE
SOUTHWARD INTO THE OZARKS AND OHIO VALLEY TODAY. TO THE SOUTH OF THE
BOUNDARY, SURFACE DEWPOINTS IN THE UPPER 60S TO LOWER 70S F WILL
LIKELY RESULT IN POCKETS OF MODERATE INSTABILITY BY AFTERNOON. IN
SPITE OF LIMITED LARGE-SCALE ASCENT, INCREASING LOW-LEVEL
CONVERGENCE NEAR THE BOUNDARY WILL RESULT IN SCATTERED CONVECTIVE
INITIATION. DEEP-LAYER SHEAR NEAR THE FRONT MAY BE STRONG ENOUGH FOR
A FEW ISOLATED SEVERE STORMS IN AREAS THAT DESTABILIZE THE MOST. THE
PRIMARY THREAT WILL BE DAMAGING WIND GUSTS.

..BROYLES/CHALMERS.. 10/14/2026

...THERE IS A MARGINAL RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS OVER PORTIONS OF
THE MID-MISSISSIPPI AND OHIO VALLEYS...

...SUMMARY...
ISOLATED SEVERE STORMS CAPABLE OF DAMAGING WIND GUSTS AND PERHAPS A
BRIEF TORNADO OR TWO ARE POSSIBLE OVER PORTIONS OF THE
MID-MISSISSIPPI AND OHIO VALLEYS THURSDAY AFTERNOON AND EVENING.

...SYNOPSIS...

A SHORT-WAVE TROUGH OVER CENTRAL CANADA THURSDAY MORNING IS FORECAST
TO UNDERGO CONSIDERABLE AMPLIFICATION WHILE TRANSLATING INTO THE
GREAT LAKES LATE THURSDAY NIGHT/EARLY FRIDAY. AHEAD OF THAT FEATURE,
AN UPPER LOW WILL WEAKEN INTO AN INCREASINGLY SHEARED OPEN WAVE
WHILE ACCELERATING FROM THE CENTRAL HIGH PLAINS INTO THE MIDWEST.
THAT PATTERN EVOLUTION WILL YIELD A STEADILY STRENGTHENING
DEEP-LAYER WIND FIELD OVER THE MID-MS AND OH VALLEYS THROUGH THE
FORECAST PERIOD. AT THE SURFACE, AN AREA OF LOW PRESSURE IS EXPECTED
TO DEVELOP ALONG A WARM FRONT FROM CENTRAL OR EASTERN KS THROUGH THE
MID-MS AND OH VALLEYS TO THE LOWER GREAT LAKES. MEANWHILE, A
TRAILING COLD FRONT WILL SURGE SOUTH THROUGH MUCH OF THE SOUTHERN
PLAINS, OZARK PLATEAU, AND MID-MS VALLEY.


...MID-MISSISSIPPI AND OHIO VALLEYS...

SCATTERED TO NUMEROUS THUNDERSTORMS ARE EXPECTED TO BE ONGOING
THURSDAY MORNING FROM THE CENTRAL PLAINS INTO THE MID-MS VALLEY
AHEAD OF THE SURFACE LOW AND WITHIN A ZONE OF LOW-LEVEL WARM
ADVECTION AND THE POSSIBLE INFLUENCE OF A LEAD DISTURBANCE MOVING
THROUGH THE REGION. A SUBSET OF THE MORNING STORMS IS LIKELY TO
CONTINUE THROUGH THE REMAINDER OF THE DAY WITH CONVECTIVE OUTFLOW
AND CLOUDS LIMITING AIR MASS DESTABILIZATION IN THOSE AREAS.

A RELATIVELY NARROW PLUME OF UPPER 60S TO LOW 70S F DEWPOINTS WILL
BE ADVECTED INTO THE NORTHERN EXTENSION OF THE SYSTEM WARM SECTOR,
CONTRIBUTING TO MLCAPE UP TO 500-1000 J/KG BY MID TO LATE AFTERNOON
IN AREAS PERIPHERAL TO ACTIVE CONVECTION. A SUBSEQUENT INCREASE IN
SURFACE-BASED STORM COVERAGE AND INTENSITY IS EXPECTED AT THAT TIME
FROM EAST-CENTRAL MO INTO CENTRAL AND SOUTHERN IL. THE PRESENCE OF
40-50+ KT OF DEEP-LAYER SHEAR WILL BE SUPPORTIVE OF TRANSIENT
SUPERCELL AND BOWING STRUCTURES CAPABLE OF ISOLATED DAMAGING WIND
GUSTS AND PERHAPS A BRIEF TORNADO OR TWO THROUGH THE EVENING.

THE DURATION AND EASTWARD EXTENT OF THE DAMAGING WIND THREAT REMAIN
IN QUESTION THURSDAY NIGHT IN THE MID TO UPPER-OH VALLEY. MODEL
SOUNDINGS INDICATE A SHALLOW, NEAR-GROUND INVERSION DEVELOPING
WITHIN THE WARM SECTOR AS THE SURFACE LOW OUTPACES BETTER LOW-LEVEL
MOISTURE RETURN. HOWEVER, WIDELY SPACED DAMAGING WIND GUSTS MAY
STILL REMAIN POSSIBLE GIVEN THE PRESENCE OF 40-50 KT WINDS WITHIN
THE LOWEST KM ABOVE GROUND.


...SOUTHERN PLAINS/OZARK PLATEAU...

A FEW STRONG STORMS CAPABLE OF GUSTY WINDS AND/OR SMALL HAIL APPEAR
POSSIBLE THURSDAY AFTERNOON AND EVENING ALONG THE COLD FRONT
PROGRESSING THROUGH THE REGION. POOR MIDLEVEL LAPSE RATES, WEAK
LOW-LEVEL WINDS, AND DEEP-LAYER SHEAR VECTORS ORIENTED PARALLEL TO
THE PROGRESSIVE/UNDERCUTTING FRONT ARE LIKELY TO LEAD TO
OUTFLOW-DOMINANT, DISORGANIZED CONVECTIVE MODES, LIMITING SEVERE
WEATHER POTENTIAL.

...THERE IS A MARGINAL RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS ACROSS PORTIONS
OF THE MID-ATLANTIC...

...SUMMARY...
ISOLATED SEVERE STORMS ARE POSSIBLE ACROSS PORTIONS OF THE
MID-ATLANTIC FRIDAY AFTERNOON AND EVENING.

...SYNOPSIS...

AN INTENSE TROUGH AND ASSOCIATED 90-100 KT MIDLEVEL JET ARE EXPECTED
TO PROGRESS FROM THE GREAT LAKES AND MID-MS VALLEY FRIDAY MORNING TO
THE APPALACHIANS BY LATE FRIDAY NIGHT/EARLY SATURDAY. AT THE
SURFACE, AN AREA OF LOW PRESSURE WILL DEVELOP FROM THE LOWER GREAT
LAKES TO THE GULF OF ST. LAWRENCE WHILE A TRAILING COLD FRONT
ADVANCES THROUGH NEW ENGLAND, THE MID-ATLANTIC, AND THE SOUTHEAST,
EXCLUDING CENTRAL/SOUTH FL.


...MID-ATLANTIC...

EARLY-DAY SHOWERS AND THUNDERSTORMS APPEAR PROBABLE ACROSS PORTIONS
OF THE REGION, ESPECIALLY NORTH OF THE MASON-DIXON LINE. CLOUDS AND
AREAS OF PRECIPITATION MAY LINGER INTO THE AFTERNOON AT SOME
LOCATIONS, BUT WHERE STRONGER HEATING CAN OCCUR, THE PRESENCE OF
BOUNDARY-LAYER DEWPOINTS IN THE 60S TO AROUND 70 F IS EXPECTED TO
YIELD AT LEAST MARGINAL INSTABILITY WITHIN THE WARM SECTOR. THAT AIR
MASS DESTABILIZATION WILL COINCIDE WITH INCREASING HEIGHT FALLS
ALOFT, LIKELY YIELDING AN UPTICK IN STORM COVERAGE AND INTENSITY
THROUGH THE AFTERNOON ALONG AND AHEAD OF THE COLD FRONT. THE
PRESENCE OF A STRONGLY SHEARED ENVIRONMENT WILL BE SUPPORTIVE OF
ORGANIZED STORM MODES WITH AN ASSOCIATED RISK FOR ISOLATED DAMAGING
WIND GUSTS INTO THE EVENING.

0325 AM CDT WED OCT 14 2026

VALID 171200Z - 221200Z

...DISCUSSION...
...DAYS 4 THROUGH 6/SATURDAY THROUGH MONDAY...

AN INTENSE MIDLEVEL TROUGH WILL MOVE OFF THE ATLANTIC COAST EARLY ON
DAY 4/SATURDAY IN RESPONSE TO STRONG ZONAL FLOW DEVELOPING ALONG THE
U.S./CANADIAN BORDER. ELSEWHERE, AN UPPER LOW OFF THE BAJA COAST
WILL WEAKEN WHILE ACCELERATING THROUGH THE SOUTHWEST INTO THE GREAT
PLAINS. IN THE LOW LEVELS, A DRIER AND MORE STABLE AIR MASS IS
EXPECTED TO LIMIT SEVERE WEATHER POTENTIAL THROUGH AT LEAST MONDAY.


...DAYS 7 AND 8/TUESDAY AND WEDNESDAY...

THERE IS GOOD MODEL AGREEMENT THAT A MIDLEVEL TROUGH WILL MOVE
INLAND FROM THE PACIFIC COAST. MEANWHILE, AN ASSOCIATED DEEPENING OF
A LEE SURFACE TROUGH IS EXPECTED OVER THE HIGH PLAINS, WHICH IN TURN
WILL PROMOTE THE POLEWARD FLUX OF AN INCREASINGLY MOIST AIR MASS
FROM THE GULF INTO THE GREAT PLAINS. AN ISOLATED SEVERE WEATHER
THREAT COULD MATERIALIZE BY AS EARLY AS TUESDAY IN THE
LOWER-COLORADO VALLEY, WHERE STRENGTHENING VERTICAL SHEAR AND
FORCING FOR ASCENT OVERSPREAD A MARGINALLY UNSTABLE ENVIRONMENT.
UNCERTAINTY REMAINS IN HOW QUICKLY THE MIDLEVEL TROUGH WILL MOVE
THROUGH THE INTERIOR WEST; HOWEVER, IT DOES APPEAR THAT SEVERE
WEATHER POTENTIAL MAY INCREASE OVER THE CENTRAL U.S. BY WEDNESDAY OR
THURSDAY OF NEXT WEEK.

DAY 1 FIRE WEATHER OUTLOOK
NWS STORM PREDICTION CENTER NORMAN OK
1256 AM CDT WED OCT 14 2026

VALID 141200Z - 151200Z

...NO CRITICAL AREAS...

...SYNOPSIS...
A MID-LEVEL LOW OVER THE FOUR CORNERS REGION WILL SEND AN EASTWARD
PROGRESSING COLD FRONT INTO WESTERN NM TODAY. HIGH ELEVATION SNOW IS
LIKELY IN SOUTHWESTERN TO CENTRAL CO WITH PRECIPITATION CHANCES
SPREADING INTO THE CENTRAL PLAINS AS THE TROUGH LIFTS OVER NORTHERN
CO. FARTHER WEST, AN OFFSHORE GRADIENT WILL BEGIN TO DEVELOP THIS
AFTERNOON IN SOUTHERN CA, INCREASING EAST/NORTHEAST SUSTAINED WINDS
TO 15-20 MPH AMID 15-20% RH IN TERRAIN-FAVORED GAPS OF WESTERN
L.A./VENTURA/SANTA BARBARA COUNTIES; THUS, LOCALLY ELEVATED FIRE
WEATHER CONDITIONS ARE POSSIBLE WHERE POCKETS OF DRY FUELS EXIST.
THE PRESSURE GRADIENT WILL STRENGTHEN LATE IN THE FORECAST PERIOD
(EARLY THURSDAY MORNING) LEADING INTO AN OFFSHORE WIND EVENT ON DAY
2/THURSDAY.

MEANWHILE, HIGH PRESSURE WILL TRANSITION OVER THE SOUTHEAST,
TRANSPORTING RICH GULF MOISTURE INTO THE SOUTHERN PLAINS AND OZARKS.
AS A RESULT, ANOMALOUSLY HIGH PWATS RANGING FROM 1.8-2.2" AND
AFTERNOON LARGE SCALE ASCENT WILL PROMOTE AREAS OF HEAVY RAINFALL
ALONG A SAGGING COLD FRONT. THIS SYSTEM IS EXPECTED TO PROVIDE
RELIEF TO DRYING FUELS ACROSS THE REGION, ESPECIALLY TO AREAS
CATEGORIZED BY EXTREME TO EXCEPTIONAL DROUGHT.

..ELIZALDE-GARCIA.. 10/14/2026

NWS STORM PREDICTION CENTER NORMAN OK
0105 AM CDT WED OCT 14 2026

VALID 151200Z - 161200Z

...SYNOPSIS...
A MID-LEVEL LOW OVER THE CENTRAL PLAINS WILL WEAKEN AND TRANSITION
TO AN OPEN WAVE, QUICKLY BECOMING ABSORBED WITHIN DEFINED TROUGHING
OVER THE UPPER MIDWEST ON DAY 2/THURSDAY. MEANWHILE, A CUTOFF LOW
WILL DETACH INTO THE DESERT SOUTHWEST AS RIDGING AMPLIFIES OVER THE
PACIFIC NORTHWEST. AS A RESULT, AN INCREASING OFFSHORE PRESSURE
GRADIENT WILL PROMOTE AN ELEVATED FIRE WEATHER THREAT FOR PORTIONS
OF SOUTHERN CA THAT HAVE NOT SEEN RECENT RAINFALL.

...SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA...
OFFSHORE WINDS AND FIRE WEATHER IMPACTS WILL INCREASE ACROSS
SOUTHERN CA AS A MID-LEVEL CUTOFF LOW APPROACHES THE BAJA PENINSULA,
PLACING EAST-NORTHEAST FLOW OVER THE REGION. EAST-NORTHEAST
SUSTAINED WINDS OF 15-25 MPH (GUSTS OF 30-50 MPH IN VALLEYS,
POSSIBLY UP TO 60 MPH IN THE MOUNTAINS) AMID RH AS LOW AS 10% ARE
EXPECTED TO BEGIN DAY 2/THURSDAY MORNING IN WESTERN L.A. AND VENTURA
COUNTIES. LOCALIZED CRITICAL FIRE WEATHER CONDITIONS ARE POSSIBLE IN
WIND-PRONE CORRIDORS, BUT FORECAST ERCS ONLY IN THE 60-70TH
PERCENTILE RANGE SHOULD ATTENUATE BROADER IMPACTS. POOR OVERNIGHT
HUMIDITY RECOVERIES ARE EXPECTED WITH PERSISTING STRONG WINDS,
YIELDING CONTINUED FIRE WEATHER IMPACTS INTO DAY 3/FRIDAY. ELEVATED
HIGHLIGHTS WERE INTRODUCED WHERE RAINFALL-NEGLECTED FUELS MAY BE
RECEPTIVE TO NEW FIRE STARTS.

ON THE NORTHERN PERIPHERY OF THE MID-LEVEL LOW OVER SOUTHERN CA,
EASTERLY WINDS ALOFT WILL CONTRIBUTE TO PERIODS OF GUSTY SURFACE
WINDS AMID 10-15% RH IN THE SACRAMENTO VALLEY. NORTH TO EAST WINDS
IN THE VALLEY AND ADJACENT FOOTHILLS MAY APPROACH 10-15 MPH, PERHAPS
UP TO 20 MPH IN TERRAIN-FAVORED AREAS, PROMOTING LOCALLY ELEVATED
FIRE WEATHER CONDITIONS. STRONGER WIND GUSTS OF 30-40 MPH ARE
EXPECTED IN THE HIGHER ELEVATION SIERRA; HOWEVER, MODEST RH SHOULD
ALLEVIATE BROADER FIRE WEATHER CONCERNS.

..ELIZALDE-GARCIA.. 10/14/2026
