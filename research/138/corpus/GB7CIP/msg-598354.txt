OFFICIAL ARISS ANNOUNCEMENT - ISS SSTV SERIES 34 TRANSMISSION EVENT

SPACE-RADIO AND EDUCATIONAL PROGRAMS TECHNICAL BULLETIN
EVENT DATES: OCTOBER 22 TO OCTOBER 25, 2026
VALID: 19-OCT-2026 ONWARDS
ISSUED VIA BPQ32 NODE HP2DFA-5 IN PANAMA

======================================================================
1. SSTV SERIES 34 EVENT OVERVIEW
======================================================================
AMATEUR RADIO ON THE INTERNATIONAL SPACE STATION (ARISS) HAS ANNOUNCED A NEW
SLOW-SCAN TELEVISION (SSTV) TRANSMISSION EVENT FROM THE ISS. THIS IS **SSTV
SERIES 34**, WHICH WILL FEATURE A SPECIAL COLLECTION OF IMAGES HIGHLIGHTING
ARISS HARDWARE AND ONBOARD EQUIPMENT.

======================================================================
2. TECHNICAL PARAMETERS & SCHEDULE
======================================================================
- **TRANSMISSION DATES**    : OCTOBER 22 THROUGH OCTOBER 25, 2026 (UTC).
- **DOWNLINK FREQUENCY**    : 437.500 MHZ ( /- DOPPLER SHIFT).
- **TRANSMISSION MODE**     : ROBOT 36.
- **IMAGE SUBMISSION**      : DECODED AND RECEIVED IMAGES MAY BE SUBMITTED TO THE
                            OFFICIAL ARISS SSTV GALLERY ONLINE.

======================================================================
3. RECEPTION RECOMMENDATIONS FOR OPERATORS
======================================================================
AMATEUR OPERATORS AND SHORTWAVE LISTENERS ARE ENCOURAGED TO TUNE THEIR UHF
STATIONS TO 437.500 MHZ DURING ISS PASSES. USING DIRECTIONAL ANTENNAS, TRACKING
DOPPLER SHIFT, AND RUNNING SSTV DECODING SOFTWARE (SUCH AS MMSSTV) SET TO ROBOT 36
MODE WILL ENSURE CLEAR RECEPTION OF THE ARISS HARDWARE IMAGE SERIES.
