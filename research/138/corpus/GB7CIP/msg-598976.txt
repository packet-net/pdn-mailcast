WATCHES AND WARNINGS
--------------------
There are no coastal watches or warnings in effect.

Interests along the southwestern coast of Mexico should monitor the
progress of Vance.


DISCUSSION AND OUTLOOK
----------------------
At 300 AM CST (0900 UTC), the center of Tropical Storm Vance was
located near latitude 15.3 North, longitude 105.1 West.  Vance is
moving toward the northwest near 6 mph (9 km/h).  A
west-northwestward motion is expected over the next day or so,
followed by a turn toward the north around midweek.

Maximum sustained winds are near 65 mph (100 km/h) with higher
gusts.  Gradual weakening is forecast for the next several days.

Tropical-storm-force winds extend outward up to 140 miles (220 km)
from the center.

The estimated minimum central pressure is 995 mb (29.39 inches).
