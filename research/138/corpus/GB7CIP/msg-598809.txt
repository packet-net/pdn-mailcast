WATCHES AND WARNINGS
--------------------
There are no coastal watches or warnings in effect.

Interests along the southwestern coast of Mexico should monitor the
progress of Vance. A Tropical Storm Watch may be required for
portions of the southwestern coast of Mexico tonight or on Sunday.


DISCUSSION AND OUTLOOK
----------------------
At 300 PM CST (2100 UTC), the center of Hurricane Vance was located
near latitude 13.3 North, longitude 103.3 West. Vance is moving
toward the north-northwest near 6 mph (9 km/h), and a slow motion
toward the north-northwest is expected to continue through Monday.

Maximum sustained winds have decreased to near 90 mph (150 km/h)
with higher gusts.  Additional weakening is expected through Sunday
night. Some re-intensification is possible on Monday.

Hurricane-force winds extend outward up to 30 miles (45 km) from the
center and tropical-storm-force winds extend outward up to 150 miles
(240 km).

The estimated minimum central pressure is 977 mb (28.85 inches).
