WATCHES AND WARNINGS

There are no coastal watches or warnings in effect.


DISCUSSION AND OUTLOOK
----------------------
At 800 AM MST (1500 UTC), the center of Tropical Storm Trudy was
located near latitude 19.5 North, longitude 108.5 West. Trudy is
moving toward the west-southwest near 7 mph (11 km/h). A westward to
west-southwestward motion is expected through tonight, followed by a
gradual turn toward the northwest on Tuesday.

Maximum sustained winds are near 60 mph (95 km/h) with higher gusts.
Weakening is expected over the next couple of days, and Trudy is
forecast to degenerate to a post-tropical cyclone by early Tuesday.

Tropical-storm-force winds extend outward up to 70 miles (110 km)
from the center.

The estimated minimum central pressure is 999 mb (29.50 inches).


HAZARDS AFFECTING LAND
----------------------
SURF: Swells generated by Trudy will continue affecting portions of
the coast of southwestern and west-central Mexico today. These
swells are likely to cause life-threatening surf and rip current
conditions. Please consult products from your local weather office.
