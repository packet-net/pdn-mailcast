DISCUSSION AND OUTLOOK
----------------------
At 900 PM CST (0300 UTC), the center of Hurricane Vance was located
near latitude 13.7 North, longitude 103.4 West. Vance is moving
slowly toward the north-northwest near 5 mph (7 km/h), and this
general motion is expected to continue through Monday.

Maximum sustained winds remain near 90 mph (150 km/h) with higher
gusts. Fluctuations in strength are likely through Monday, followed
by steady weakening after that.

Hurricane-force winds extend outward up to 30 miles (45 km) from the
center and tropical-storm-force winds extend outward up to 150 miles
(240 km).

The estimated minimum central pressure is 977 mb (28.85 inches).
