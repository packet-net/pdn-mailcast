DISCUSSION AND OUTLOOK
----------------------
At 900 AM CST (1500 UTC), the center of Tropical Depression
Twenty-Two-E was located near latitude 9.5 North, longitude 99.3
West. The depression is moving toward the west near 7 mph (11 km/h)
and this general slow forward motion with a turn towards the
northwest then north-northwest is expected the next few days.

Maximum sustained winds are near 35 mph (55 km/h) with higher gusts.
Steady to rapid intensification is expected, and the system is
forecast to become a hurricane on Friday.

The estimated minimum central pressure is 1005 mb (29.68 inches).


HAZARDS AFFECTING LAND
----------------------
SURF: Swells generated by Tropical Depression Twenty-Two-E are
expected to begin affecting portions of the coast of southern and
southwestern Mexico this weekend and continue into next week.  These
swells are likely to cause life-threatening surf and rip current
conditions.  Please consult products from your local weather office.
