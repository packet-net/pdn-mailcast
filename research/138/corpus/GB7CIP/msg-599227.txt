256 AM EDT Sat Oct 24 2026

...There is a Slight Risk (level 2/4) of excessive rainfall over
parts of the Central/Southern High Plains and Central/Southern
Rockies on Saturday and Sunday...

...There is a Slight Risk (level 2/4) of excessive rainfall over
parts of Southeastern Florida on Sunday...

...There is a Slight Risk (level 2/5) of severe thunderstorms over
parts of the Southwest/Southern Rockies on Saturday...

An upper-level low over Southern California and the Southwest by
Saturday and will be quasi-stationary through Sunday morning.
Moisture from the Gulf of California will stream into the
Southwest/Southern High Plains, producing showers and severe
thunderstorms. Therefore, the SPC has issued a Slight Risk (level
2/5) of severe thunderstorms over parts of the Southern High
Plains and the Southwest through early Sunday morning. The hazards
associated with these thunderstorms are frequent lightning, severe
thunderstorm wind gusts, hail, and a few tornadoes.

In addition, the showers and thunderstorms will produce heavy
rain. Therefore, the WPC has issued a Slight Risk (level 2/4) of
excessive rainfall with these thunderstorms over parts of the
Central/Southern High Plains and Central/Southern Rockies on
Saturday. The associated heavy rain will create mainly localized
areas of flash flooding, with urban areas, roads, small streams,
narrow canyons/gullies, and burn scars the most vulnerable.

Overnight Saturday, a front over the Eastern Pacific will move
onshore over the Pacific Northwest, producing rain over parts of
the Pacific Northwest late Saturday night and early Sunday. The
rain will continue over the region through Monday.

Moreover, on Sunday, showers and thunderstorms with heavy rain
will continue to develop over parts of the Central/Southern High
Plains. Therefore, the WPC has issued a Slight Risk (level 2/4) of
excessive rainfall with these thunderstorms over parts of the
Central/Southern High Plains. The associated heavy rain will
create mainly localized areas of flash flooding, with urban areas,
roads, small streams, narrow canyons/gullies, and burn scars the
most vulnerable.

Also on Sunday, easterly flow off the Atlantic will produce
showers and thunderstorms with heavy rain over the eastern coast
of Florida. Therefore, the WPC has issued a Slight Risk (level
2/4) of excessive rainfall with these thunderstorms over parts of
the eastern coast of Florida. The associated heavy rain will
create mainly localized areas of flash flooding, with urban areas,
roads, small streams, and low-lying areas being the most
vulnerable.

By Monday, the threat of excessive rainfall will decrease to a
Marginal Risk (level 1/4) of excessive rainfall over parts of the
Southern Plains and Florida.  The associated heavy rain will
create localized areas of flash flooding, affecting areas that
experience rapid runoff with heavy rain.
