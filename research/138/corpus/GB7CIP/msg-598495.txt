SPC MD 2384

MD 2384 CONCERNING SEVERE POTENTIAL...WATCH UNLIKELY FOR PARTS OF
CENTRAL AND NORTHERN ARIZONA


MESOSCALE DISCUSSION 2384
NWS STORM PREDICTION CENTER NORMAN OK
0305 AM CDT WED OCT 21 2026

AREAS AFFECTED...PARTS OF CENTRAL AND NORTHERN ARIZONA

CONCERNING...SEVERE POTENTIAL...WATCH UNLIKELY

VALID 210805Z - 211000Z

PROBABILITY OF WATCH ISSUANCE...5 PERCENT

SUMMARY...SCATTERED STRONG THUNDERSTORM DEVELOPMENT POSING A RISK
FOR SMALL TO MARGINALLY SEVERE HAIL MAY PERSIST ANOTHER HOUR OR TWO,
NORTH OF THE GREATER PHOENIX AREA INTO THE MOGOLLON RIM VICINITY
SOUTH OF FLAGSTAFF, WITH DIMINISHING POTENTIAL THROUGH 2-4 AM MST
AND INTO/ACROSS NORTHERN ARIZONA.

DISCUSSION...THERE HAS BEEN AN INCREASE IN NUMBER OF THUNDERSTORMS
INITIATING OVER THE PAST COUPLE OF HOURS, AS A FOCUSED AREA OF
LARGE-SCALE ASCENT BEGINS TO SPREAD NORTH OF THE LOWER DESERTS INTO
THE COLORADO PLATEAU.  AT HIGHER ELEVATIONS ACROSS AND NORTH OF THE
MOGOLLON RIM, THE BOUNDARY-LAYER MOISTURE INCREASES REMAIN MODEST,
WITHIN A RELATIVELY COOL ENVIRONMENT.  THIS IS LIKELY TO CONTINUE TO
SUPPRESS DESTABILIZATION, PARTICULARLY AS TEMPERATURES AROUND 500 MB
BEGIN TO WARM THROUGH 10-12Z.  IN THE  PRESENCE OF STRONG DEEP-LAYER
SHEAR, THE ENVIRONMENT NORTH OF GREATER PHOENIX INTO THE MOGOLLON
RIM VICINITY MAY CONTINUE TO SUPPORT A FEW STORMS CAPABLE OF
PRODUCING SMALL TO MARGINALLY SEVERE HAIL FOR ANOTHER HOUR OR TWO,
HOWEVER THIS POTENTIAL IS EXPECTED TO REMAIN MORE LIMITED WITH
NORTHWARD EXTENT.

..KERR/GLEASON.. 10/21/2026

SPC OCT 21, 2026 0600 UTC DAY 1 CONVECTIVE OUTLOOK

SPC 1200Z DAY 1 OUTLOOK

DAY 1 CONVECTIVE OUTLOOK
NWS STORM PREDICTION CENTER NORMAN OK
1251 AM CDT WED OCT 21 2026

VALID 211200Z - 221200Z

...THERE IS AN ENHANCED RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS ACROSS PARTS OF
CENTRAL AND NORTHWESTERN ARIZONA...

...SUMMARY...
SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS WITH A POTENTIAL FOR LARGE HAIL, SEVERE WIND
GUSTS AND A COUPLE TORNADOES ARE EXPECTED TODAY ALONG THE MOGOLLON
RIM ACROSS PARTS OF ARIZONA. MARGINALLY SEVERE WIND GUSTS AND AN
ISOLATED TORNADO THREAT WILL ALSO BE POSSIBLE ALONG THE SOUTHERN AND
CENTRAL CALIFORNIA COAST.

...DISCUSSION...
AN UPPER-LEVEL LOW WILL APPROACH THE CALIFORNIA COAST TODAY, AS
SOUTHWESTERLY DIVERGENT FLOW ALOFT REMAINS IN PLACE OVER MUCH OF THE
SOUTHWEST. AHEAD OF THE SYSTEM, LOW-LEVEL MOISTURE WILL ADVECT
NORTHWARD INTO THE DESERT SOUTHWEST TODAY. AN AXIS WITH SURFACE
DEWPOINTS IN THE 60S F WILL SET UP TO THE SOUTHWEST OF THE MOGOLLON
RIM ACROSS SOUTHERN, CENTRAL AND WESTERN ARIZONA. BY AFTERNOON,
MLCAPE IS FORECAST TO PEAK IN THE 1500 TO 2000 J/KG RANGE ALONG AND
NEAR THE MOIST AXIS. OVER THE TOP OF THE MOIST AND UNSTABLE AIRMASS,
STRONG MID-LEVEL SOUTHWESTERLY FLOW WILL BE IN PLACE. MODERATE
INSTABILITY AND STRONG DEEP-LAYER SHEAR WILL SUPPORT A SUBSTANTIAL
SEVERE THREAT WITH STORMS THAT DEVELOP ALONG THE MOGOLLON RIM FROM
THIS AFTERNOON INTO THE EVENING, NEAR THE NORTHERN EDGE OF THE
STRONGER INSTABILITY.

AS THE UPPER-LEVEL SYSTEM APPROACHES, A 50 TO 70 KNOT MID-LEVEL JET
WILL OVERSPREAD THE DESERT SOUTHWEST TODAY. RAP FORECAST SOUNDINGS
WITHIN THE EXIT REGION OF THE JET OVER NORTH-CENTRAL ARIZONA IN THE
MID TO LATE AFTERNOON HAVE GRADUALLY VEERING WINDS WITH HEIGHT AND
50 TO 60 KNOTS OF 0-6 KM SHEAR. IN ADDITION, STEEP MID-LEVEL LAPSE
RATES AND LONG HODOGRAPHS ARE EVIDENT WHICH WILL RESULT IN A
POTENTIAL FOR SUPERCELLS WITH LARGE HAIL. THE STRONGEST CELLS COULD
PRODUCE HAILSTONES GREATER THAN 2 INCHES IN DIAMETER. FORECAST
SOUNDINGS ALSO HAVE 0-3 KM STORM-RELATIVE HELICITY IN THE 175 TO 250
M2/S2 RANGE LATE THIS AFTERNOON ALONG PARTS OF THE MOGOLLON RIM
SUGGESTING THAT THE STRONGER SUPERCELLS WILL BE CAPABLE OF
TORNADOES. THE SEVERE THREAT IS EXPECTED TO PERSIST FROM MID
AFTERNOON INTO THE EVENING, WITH A POTENTIAL FOR TWO ROUNDS OF
SEVERE STORMS IMPACTING PARTS OF NORTH-CENTRAL ARIZONA.

...COAST OF SOUTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA...
A LARGE UPPER-LEVEL LOW PRESSURE SYSTEM WILL MOVE TOWARD THE
CALIFORNIA COAST TODAY. THE LEFT EXIT REGION OF A MID-LEVEL JET WILL
APPROACH THE SOUTHERN AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA COAST. A WAVE OF
PRECIPITATION ASSOCIATED WITH THIS FEATURE WILL MOVE TO THE SOUTHERN
AND CENTRAL CALIFORNIA COAST THIS AFTERNOON. INSTABILITY ALONG THE
COAST WILL BE SUFFICIENT FOR THUNDERSTORM DEVELOPMENT. RAP FORECAST
SOUNDINGS ALONG THE COAST THIS AFTERNOON HAVE STRONG DEEP-LAYER
SHEAR, WHICH WILL SUPPORT THE DEVELOPMENT OF LOW-TOPPED SUPERCELLS.
SOME MODEL FORECASTS HAVE 0-3 KM STORM-RELATIVE HELICITY AROUND 200
M2/S2 NEAR THE COAST SUGGESTING THAT AN ISOLATED TORNADO THREAT WILL
BE POSSIBLE. ANY CELLS THAT ROTATE AND MOVE ONSHORE COULD ALSO BE
ASSOCIATED WITH ISOLATED SEVERE WIND GUSTS AS WELL.

..BROYLES/LEITMAN.. 10/21/2026

SPC OCT 21, 2026 0600 UTC DAY 2 CONVECTIVE OUTLOOK

SPC 0600Z DAY 2 OUTLOOK

DAY 2 CONVECTIVE OUTLOOK
NWS STORM PREDICTION CENTER NORMAN OK
1247 AM CDT WED OCT 21 2026

VALID 221200Z - 231200Z

...THERE IS A SLIGHT RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS FOR PORTIONS OF
CENTRAL ARIZONA INTO FAR WESTERN NEW MEXICO...

...SUMMARY...
SCATTERED SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS ARE EXPECTED ACROSS THE SOUTHWEST AND
FOUR CORNERS AREA ON THURSDAY, WITH THE GREATEST RISK ACROSS PARTS
OF ARIZONA. ALL SEVERE HAZARDS WILL BE POSSIBLE.

...SYNOPSIS...
A BROAD LONGWAVE MID-LEVEL TROUGH WILL TRAVERSE THE WESTERN CONUS ON
THURSDAY, ENCOURAGING CONTINUED DEEP-LAYER ASCENT AMID THE NORTHWARD
ADVECTION OF LOW-LEVEL MOISTURE, PROMOTING SCATTERED TO WIDESPREAD
THUNDERSTORM DEVELOPMENT. AT LEAST ISOLATED SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS ARE
POSSIBLE SPANNING THE DESERT SOUTHWEST INTO THE GREAT BASIN GIVEN
OVERLAPPING MARGINAL BUOYANCY AND WIDESPREAD STRONG WIND FIELDS
ALOFT. HOWEVER, ACROSS PORTIONS OF THE DESERT SOUTHWEST, AN EMBEDDED
MID-LEVEL IMPULSE ACCOMPANIED BY A 500 MB SPEED MAX ATOP LOCALLY
HIGHER LOW-LEVEL MOISTURE/INSTABILITY MAY PROMOTE A MORE FOCUSED
RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS.

...DESERT SOUTHWEST INTO THE GREAT BASIN...
DEEP-LAYER ASCENT, COMBINED WITH AT LEAST SOME DEGREE OF SURFACE
HEATING AHEAD OF THE BROADER UPPER TROUGH, WILL SUPPORT SCATTERED
THUNDERSTORM DEVELOPMENT OVER THE GREAT BASIN DURING THE AFTERNOON.
LARGELY UNIDIRECTIONAL (SOUTHERLY) TROPOSPHERIC FLOW WILL YIELD
HIGHLY ELONGATED, STRAIGHT HODOGRAPHS AND OVER 50 KTS OF EFFECTIVE
BULK (SPEED) SHEAR. GIVEN 7 C/KM MID-LEVEL LAPSE RATES AND AROUND
500 J/KG MLCAPE, TRANSIENT SUPERCELLS AND SMALL LINE SEGMENTS WILL
BE THE MAIN STORM MODES, ACCOMPANIED BY AN ISOLATED SEVERE GUST/HAIL
THREAT.

FARTHER SOUTH INTO CENTRAL AZ TO FAR WESTERN NM, A 70+ KT 500 MB
SOUTHWESTERLY SPEED MAX WITH 7+ C/KM MID-LEVEL LAPSE RATES WILL
OVERSPREAD SOUTHEASTERLY LOW-LEVEL FLOW AND LOW TO MID 60S F SURFACE
DEWPOINTS. THIS AMBIENT ENVIRONMENT, AS SHOWN BY FORECAST SOUNDINGS,
WILL YIELD MODESTLY ELONGATED HODOGRAPHS, BUT WITH APPRECIABLE SIZE
AND CURVATURE AT THE LOW-LEVELS. GIVEN LOCALLY HIGHER MOISTURE, UP
TO 1500 J/KG MLCAPE MAY BE REALIZED IN TANDEM WITH 50+ KTS OF
EFFECTIVE BULK SHEAR AND OVER 200 M2/S2 OF EFFECTIVE SRH. WHILE CAM
GUIDANCE CONSENSUS DEPICTS A COMPLEX STORM MODE AND SEVERAL
POTENTIAL CELLULAR INTERACTIONS, LONGER-LIVED, EMBEDDED SUPERCELL
STRUCTURES ARE POSSIBLE. SHOULD SUCH STORMS DEVELOP, ALL SEVERE
HAZARDS WILL BE POSSIBLE, INCLUDING A COUPLE OF TORNADOES.

..SQUITIERI.. 10/21/2026

...THERE IS A MARGINAL RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS PORTIONS OF
CENTRAL INTO SOUTHERN NEW MEXICO...

...SUMMARY...
ISOLATED SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS ARE POSSIBLE ACROSS PORTIONS OF
CENTRAL INTO SOUTHERN NEW MEXICO ON FRIDAY.

...SYNOPSIS...
A BROAD LONGWAVE UPPER TROUGH WILL IMPINGE ON THE ROCKIES ON FRIDAY,
SUPPORTING STRONG SURFACE LEE TROUGHING ALONG THE HIGH PLAINS.
CONTINUED NORTHWARD ADVECTING LOW-LEVEL MOISTURE AND DEEP-LAYER
ASCENT ACCOMPANYING THE BROAD UPPER TROUGH WILL SUPPORT ISOLATED TO
SCATTERED THUNDERSTORMS ALL ALONG THE ROCKIES TO THE NORTHERN
PLAINS. ACROSS PORTIONS OF THE SOUTHERN ROCKIES, LOCALLY RICHER
MOISTURE AND ASSOCIATED INSTABILITY, BENEATH STRONGER FLOW ALOFT,
WILL SUPPORT A RISK FOR ISOLATED SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS.

...PORTIONS OF CENTRAL INTO SOUTHERN NEW MEXICO...
AN EMBEDDED MID-LEVEL IMPULSE AND ACCOMPANYING 50+ KT MID-LEVEL
SOUTHWESTERLY SPEED MAX WILL GRAZE THE SOUTHERN ROCKIES DURING BY
AFTERNOON. BY THIS TIME, SURFACE HEATING OF A LOW-LEVEL AIRMASS
COMPRISING UPPER 50S F DEWPOINTS AND OVERSPREAD BY 7.5+ C/KM
MID-LEVEL LAPSE RATES WILL YIELD OVER 1500 J/KG MLCAPE IN SPOTS.
SCATTERED THUNDERSTORMS SHOULD INITIATE BY AFTERNOON, AMID VEERING
AND STRENGTHENING FLOW WITH HEIGHT. FORECAST SOUNDINGS SHOW MODEST
LOW-LEVEL HODOGRAPH CURVATURE AND UP TO 40 KTS OF EFFECTIVE BULK
SHEAR. THIS SHEAR, ALONG WITH THE AFOREMENTIONED BUOYANCY, SHOULD
SUPPORT A MIX OF MULTICELLS AND SUPERCELLS CAPABLE OF PRODUCING
ISOLATED INSTANCES OF SEVERE HAIL AND WIND.

SPC OCT 21, 2026 DAY 4-8 SEVERE WEATHER OUTLOOK

DAY 4-8 OUTLOOK

DAY 4-8 CONVECTIVE OUTLOOK
NWS STORM PREDICTION CENTER NORMAN OK
0300 AM CDT WED OCT 21 2026

VALID 241200Z - 291200Z

...DISCUSSION...
A MID-LEVEL TROUGH GRADUALLY PROGRESSING ACROSS THE ROCKIES THIS
WEEKEND WILL EJECT INTO THE PLAINS, WITH THE PREVAILING MID-LEVEL
FLOW PATTERN BECOMING MORE ZONAL AND PROGRESSIVE THROUGH NEXT WEEK.
SURFACE HIGH PRESSURE AND ATTENDANT SUBSIDENCE WILL OVERSPREAD MOST
OF THE CONUS EAST OF THE ROCKIES, LIMITING THUNDERSTORM DEVELOPMENT
THROUGH AT LEAST EARLY NEXT WEEK. THE BEST CHANCE FOR ANY SEVERE
WEATHER (ALBEIT SPARSE) WOULD BE ACROSS THE SOUTHERN ROCKIES DAY 4
(SATURDAY), WHERE A BUOYANT AIRMASS WILL RESIDE AHEAD OF THE UPPER
TROUGH PRIOR TO EJECTION.

MEDIUM RANGE GUIDANCE DEPICTS THE EASTWARD PROGRESSION AND
AMPLIFICATION OF ANOTHER UPPER TROUGH TOWARD THE MIDDLE OF NEXT
WEEK, WHICH SHOULD ENCOURAGE STRONG SURFACE TROUGHING AND THE
PROGRESSION OF A COLD FRONT ACROSS THE CENTRAL U.S. THE SOUTHERN
EXTENT OF THE FRONT MAY INTERACT WITH A POTENTIALLY BUOYANT AIRMASS
OVER THE SOUTHERN PLAINS, MOST LIKELY AROUND DAY 8 (WEDNESDAY) OR
BEYOND. WHILE AT LEAST ISOLATED SEVERE STORMS ARE POSSIBLE,
QUESTIONS REGARDING THE PLACEMENT AND TIMING OF THE FRONT, AND LACK
OF CONSISTENCY AMONG GUIDANCE PRECLUDES SEVERE PROBABILITIES THIS
OUTLOOK.
