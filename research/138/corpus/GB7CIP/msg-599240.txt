Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity was at low levels. Two new active regions were numbered
in the past 24h: Region 4561 (S09W70, Dro/beta) and Region 4562 (S17E57,
Axx/alpha). The C-class activity observed was from Region 4561 and
Region 4560 (N13, L=48), that rotated beyond the W limb during the
period. The strongest flare was a C1.9 at 26/0838 UTC from Region 4561.
This flare presented secondary peaks during its decay phase, including a
C1.0/Sf at 26/0933 UTC associated with a CME first seeing at CCOR-1
images at 26/0945 UTC. More data is necessary for the analysis of the
trajectory of this CME. Other coronal activity were observed near or
beyond the NE limb, but no impact near Earth is anticipated from them.

No Earth-directed CMEs were identified in available coronagraph imagery
during the day.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is likely to remain at low levels, with a slight chance
for isolated M-class flares (R1-R2, Minor-Moderate) through 27 Oct,
mostly due to the flare potential and evolution of Region 4561. Very low
levels of solar activity are expected on 28 Oct, as Region 4561 rotates
off the visible solar disk.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels, with a peak
flux of 3,000 pfu observed at 26/0925 UTC. The greater than 10 MeV
proton flux remained steady at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to continue reaching
high levels through 28 Oct. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is
anticipated to persist at background levels through 28 Oct.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters continued reflecting mostly nominal levels, with a
weak transient observed after 26/0500 UTC. Total magnetic field strength
increased from 5 nT levels to 8 nT around 26/0730 UTC, with Bz component
briefly reaching below -5 nT around 26/0810 UTC. Solar wind speeds
gradually decreased from 375 km/s to 325 km/s and phi was predominantly
oriented in the positive solar sector, with a single period of sustained
negative sector around 26/0245-0430 UTC.

.Forecast...
Nominal solar wind conditions are expected to maintain through 28 Oct.

Geospace

The geomagnetic activity was at quiet levels.

.Forecast...
The geomagnetic field is likely to remain at mostly quiet levels over
26-28 Oct.
