WW 690 SEVERE TSTM AZ 220100Z - 220900Z


URGENT - IMMEDIATE BROADCAST REQUESTED
SEVERE THUNDERSTORM WATCH NUMBER 690
NWS STORM PREDICTION CENTER NORMAN OK
600 PM MST WED OCT 21 2026

THE NWS STORM PREDICTION CENTER HAS ISSUED A

* SEVERE THUNDERSTORM WATCH FOR PORTIONS OF
  CENTRAL AND NORTHERN ARIZONA

* EFFECTIVE THIS WEDNESDAY NIGHT AND THURSDAY MORNING FROM 600 PM
  UNTIL 200 AM MST.

* PRIMARY THREATS INCLUDE...
  ISOLATED DAMAGING WIND GUSTS TO 65 MPH POSSIBLE
  ISOLATED LARGE HAIL EVENTS TO 1.5 INCHES IN DIAMETER POSSIBLE
  A TORNADO OR TWO POSSIBLE

SUMMARY...ISOLATED SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS CAPABLE OF A RISK FOR LARGE
HAIL AND SEVERE GUSTS WILL BE POSSIBLE TONIGHT ACROSS THE WATCH
AREA.  A TORNADO IS ALSO POSSIBLE WITH THE MORE INTENSE SUPERCELLS.

THE SEVERE THUNDERSTORM WATCH AREA IS APPROXIMATELY ALONG AND 60
STATUTE MILES NORTH AND SOUTH OF A LINE FROM 25 MILES WEST OF
FLAGSTAFF AZ TO 30 MILES NORTH NORTHEAST OF SHOW LOW AZ. FOR A
COMPLETE DEPICTION OF THE WATCH SEE THE ASSOCIATED WATCH OUTLINE
UPDATE (WOUS64 KWNS WOU0).

PRECAUTIONARY/PREPAREDNESS ACTIONS...

REMEMBER...A SEVERE THUNDERSTORM WATCH MEANS CONDITIONS ARE
FAVORABLE FOR SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS IN AND CLOSE TO THE WATCH AREA.
PERSONS IN THESE AREAS SHOULD BE ON THE LOOKOUT FOR THREATENING
WEATHER CONDITIONS AND LISTEN FOR LATER STATEMENTS AND POSSIBLE
WARNINGS. SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS CAN AND OCCASIONALLY DO PRODUCE
TORNADOES.

SPC MD 2395

MD 2395 CONCERNING SEVERE THUNDERSTORM WATCH 690... FOR PARTS OF
CENTRAL ARIZONA


MESOSCALE DISCUSSION 2395
NWS STORM PREDICTION CENTER NORMAN OK
0221 AM CDT THU OCT 22 2026

AREAS AFFECTED...PARTS OF CENTRAL ARIZONA

CONCERNING...SEVERE THUNDERSTORM WATCH 690...

VALID 220721Z - 220915Z

THE SEVERE WEATHER THREAT FOR SEVERE THUNDERSTORM WATCH 690
CONTINUES.

SUMMARY...SCATTERED STRONG THUNDERSTORM DEVELOPMENT REMAINS POSSIBLE
OVERNIGHT.  ALTHOUGH THE POTENTIAL FOR SEVERE WEATHER MAY CURRENTLY
BE AT A RELATIVE MINIMUM, IT APPEARS THAT THIS MAY INCREASE AGAIN BY
4-6 AM MST.  A NEW SEVERE WEATHER WATCH MAY BE NEEDED, BUT IT IS NOT
CERTAIN WHETHER THIS WILL BE BEFORE WW 690 EXPIRES AT 09Z, OR A BIT
LATER TONIGHT.

DISCUSSION...THERE HAS BEEN LITTLE CHANGE IN THE LARGE-SCALE
ENVIRONMENT THROUGH THE EVENING INTO THE OVERNIGHT HOURS, DOWNSTREAM
OF A NOTABLE MID-LEVEL TROUGH AND EMBEDDED LOW SLOWLY MIGRATING
INLAND OF THE SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA COAST.  SOUTHERLY LOW-LEVEL FLOW
IS MAINTAINING A SEASONABLY MOIST BOUNDARY-LAYER ACROSS THE LOWER
DESERTS INTO MOGOLLON RIM VICINITY, WHICH HAS BEEN SUFFICIENT TO
CONTRIBUTE TO CAPE ON THE ORDER OF 500-1000+ J/KG BENEATH MODESTLY
COOL MID-LEVEL AIR.  BENEATH STRONG SOUTH TO SOUTHWESTERLY FLOW IN
THE 500-300 MB LAYER, DEEP-LAYER SHEAR REMAINS CONDUCIVE TO
SUPERCELL DEVELOPMENT.

FORCING FOR ASCENT NEAR AND NORTH OF THE GREATER PHOENIX AREA
CONTINUES TO SUPPORT RENEWED THUNDERSTORM INITIATION, LARGELY AIDED
BY LOW-LEVEL WARM ADVECTION AND UPSLOPE FLOW INTO AND ACROSS THE
MOGOLLON RIM INTO COLORADO PLATEAU.  THIS IS LIKELY TO CONTINUE
THROUGH THE REMAINDER OF THE NIGHT.

WITH THE CONTINUED APPROACH OF THE UPSTREAM TROUGH AXIS AND MORE
SUBSTANTIVE MID-LEVEL HEIGHT FALLS, ALONG WITH PERHAPS SOME FURTHER
STRENGTHENING OF LOW-LEVEL FLOW/SHEAR, LATEST MODEL OUTPUT
(INCLUDING CONVECTION-ALLOWING AND RELATED MACHINE LEARNING
GUIDANCE) SUGGESTS THAT THE ENVIRONMENT MAY ONCE AGAIN BECOME
INCREASINGLY CONDUCIVE TO SUPERCELLS WITH POTENTIAL TO PRODUCE A
TORNADO OR TWO, IN ADDITION TO LARGE HAIL, BY 11-13Z, IF NOT
EARLIER.

..KERR.. 10/22/2026

SPC OCT 22, 2026 0600 UTC DAY 1 CONVECTIVE OUTLOOK

SPC 1200Z DAY 1 OUTLOOK

DAY 1 CONVECTIVE OUTLOOK
NWS STORM PREDICTION CENTER NORMAN OK
0100 AM CDT THU OCT 22 2026

VALID 221200Z - 231200Z

...THERE IS A SLIGHT RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS FROM CENTRAL AND
EASTERN ARIZONA INTO WESTERN NEW MEXICO...

...SUMMARY...
SCATTERED SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS ARE EXPECTED ACROSS THE SOUTHWEST AND
FOUR CORNERS AREA TODAY INTO TONIGHT WITH THE MOST INTENSE STORMS
LIKELY ACROSS PARTS OF ARIZONA AND NEW MEXICO. ALL SEVERE HAZARDS
WILL BE POSSIBLE.

...SOUTHWEST AND FOUR CORNERS...
THE DEEP UPPER LOW INITIALLY OVER THE CA COAST WILL MOVE INLAND AND
CONTINUE EASTWARD THROUGH THE DAY, SLOWLY WEAKENING INTO AN OPEN
WAVE. A BELT OF ENHANCED SOUTHERLY MID-LEVEL FLOW (70+ KT) ALONG THE
EASTERN PERIPHERY OF THE CYCLONE WILL REMAIN IN PLACE OVER THE
SOUTHWEST AND FOUR CORNERS. SEVERAL EMBEDDED SHORTWAVE PERTURBATIONS
WILL ROTATE AROUND THE CYCLONE, AIDING IN CONTINUED LOW-LEVEL
SOUTHERLY FLOW ACROSS EASTERN AZ AND SOUTHWEST NM. HERE, SURFACE
DEWPOINTS IN THE UPPER 50S TO LOW 60S F AND DIURNAL HEATING SHOULD
CONTRIBUTE TO MODERATE BUOYANCY AMID DEEP AND STRONGLY VEERING WIND
PROFILES FAVORABLE FOR SEVERE STORMS.

ONE OR MORE BANDS OF CONVECTION ARE LIKELY TO BE ONGOING ACROSS
CENTRAL/EASTERN AZ EARLY THIS MORNING ALONG A WEAK SURFACE TROUGH.
COOLING MID-LEVEL TEMPERATURES AHEAD OF THE APPROACHING LOW AND
PERSISTENT UPSLOPE FLOW ALONG THE CENTRAL AND SOUTHERN PORTIONS OF
THE MOGOLLON RIM WILL MAINTAIN BUOYANCY THROUGH THE DIURNAL MINIMUM
WITH REINTENSIFICATION OF THESE STORMS LIKELY AS HEATING DEVELOPS
OVER THE LOWER DESERTS. STORMS WILL LIKELY BE SUPERCELLULAR
INITIALLY. LARGE HAIL (PERHAPS TO AROUND 2 INCHES) AND A COUPLE OF
TORNADOES WILL BE THE MAIN CONCERN, FOCUSED WHERE THE LARGEST
BUOYANCY AND STRONGEST LOW-LEVEL SHEAR WILL OVERLAP IN SOUTHEASTERN
AZ. WITH TIME, INCREASINGLY MERIDIONAL FLOW AND NUMEROUS STORM
INTERACTIONS WILL FAVOR MORE LINEAR MODES AND PERHAPS A TRANSITION
TO A GREATER DAMAGING WIND THREAT BY LATER IN THE AFTERNOON.

FARTHER NORTH INTO THE GREAT BASIN, SUFFICIENT DESTABILIZATION FOR
MORE THAN SPORADIC SEVERE STORMS IS MUCH LESS CERTAIN OWING TO LOWER
SURFACE DEWPOINTS AND SIGNIFICANT CLOUD COVER. STILL, MODEL GUIDANCE
SHOWS A FEW HUNDRED J/KG OF MLCAPE MAY DEVELOP WITH POCKETS OF
BETTER HEATING FROM EASTERN NV, UT AND INTO SOUTHWESTERN CO. FLOW
ALOFT WILL REMAIN FAIRLY STRONG (50-60 KT) HELPING TO ELONGATE MID
AND UPPER-LEVEL HODOGRAPHS. DPVA AND UPSLOPE TRAJECTORIES SHOULD
SUPPORT NUMEROUS SHOWERS/THUNDERSTORMS WITH SOME POTENTIAL FOR
ORGANIZATION INTO CLUSTERS OR SUPERCELL STRUCTURES. DAMAGING WIND
GUSTS ARE THE MOST LIKELY HAZARD, THOUGH MARGINALLY SEVERE HAIL IS
ALSO POSSIBLE WITH ANY OF THE STRONGER ROTATING STORMS INTO THE
EVENING.

...SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA...
AS THE UPPER-LEVEL LOW MOVES OVERHEAD DURING THE AFTERNOON, COLD
MID-LEVEL TEMPERATURES (AROUND -20C AT 500 MB) AND MODEST SURFACE
HEATING WILL SUPPORT WEAK DESTABILIZATION WITH MLCAPE OF 500-750
J/KG. LOW-TOPPED CONVECTION NEAR THE COAST MAY BE ONGOING EARLY AND
CONTINUE INTO THE EARLY AFTERNOON BEFORE MID-LEVEL ASCENT BEGINS TO
DECREASE. GIVEN THE AMBIENT VORTICITY WITH THE UPPER-LOW AND LOCAL
TERRAIN EFFECTS, A BRIEF TORNADO IS POSSIBLE WITH THE STRONGEST
STORMS ALONG WITH ISOLATED WIND DAMAGE POTENTIAL. SEVERE POTENTIAL
SHOULD RAPIDLY DIMINISH AS THE UPPER TROUGH MOVES EASTWARD LATE
THROUGH THE AFTERNOON AND INTO THE EVENING.

..LYONS/JIRAK.. 10/22/2026

...SUMMARY...
ISOLATED SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS ARE POSSIBLE ACROSS PORTIONS OF NEW
MEXICO ON FRIDAY.

...SYNOPSIS...
A BROAD UPPER TROUGH WILL SLOWLY PROGRESS OVER THE ROCKIES ON
FRIDAY, WITH A LEAD MID-LEVEL IMPULSE POISED TO EJECT INTO THE
NORTHERN PLAINS WHILE A LAGGING 500 MB CUT-OFF LOW DRIFTS EASTWARD
ACROSS THE DESERT SOUTHWEST. GIVEN ADEQUATE LOW-LEVEL MOISTURE
RETURN AND DEEP-LAYER ASCENT AHEAD OF THE LONGWAVE MID-LEVEL TROUGH,
THUNDERSTORMS WILL BE POSSIBLE ACROSS MUCH OF THE ROCKIES AND THE
CENTRAL TO NORTHERN PLAINS. ISOLATED SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS ARE
POSSIBLE ACROSS PORTIONS OF NM, WHERE LOCALLY GREATER MOISTURE AND
INSTABILITY RESIDE.

...WESTERN INTO CENTRAL NEW MEXICO...
AS THE AFOREMENTIONED LEADING MID-LEVEL IMPULSE DEPARTS THE ROCKIES
AND THE CUT-OFF LOW APPROACHES NM, 50+ KT SOUTHWESTERLY 500 MB FLOW
WILL OVERSPREAD THE DESERT SOUTHWEST, WHERE LOW TO MID 50S F SURFACE
DEWPOINTS AMID SOUTHEASTERLY SURFACE FLOW WILL BE IN PLACE. GIVEN
7-7.5 C/KM MID-LEVEL LAPSE RATES, UP TO 1000 J/KG MLCAPE MAY
MANIFEST AMID APPRECIABLY CURVED AND ELONGATED HODOGRAPHS, WITH
30-40 KTS OF EFFECTIVE BULK SHEAR LIKELY. BY AFTERNOON,
THUNDERSTORMS SHOULD DEVELOP AND INTENSIFY WITH DIURNAL HEATING.
GIVEN THE AFOREMENTIONED VERTICAL SHEAR/BUOYANCY, SOME SUPERCELL
STRUCTURES SHOULD DEVELOP, ACCOMPANIED BY AN ISOLATED SEVERE
WIND/HAIL THREAT, AND A TORNADO IS ALSO POSSIBLE.

...THERE IS A MARGINAL RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS FOR CENTRAL NEW
MEXICO INTO FAR WEST TEXAS...

...SUMMARY...
ISOLATED SEVERE WIND/HAIL OR A TORNADO ARE POSSIBLE ACROSS PORTIONS
OF CENTRAL NEW MEXICO INTO FAR WEST TEXAS ON SATURDAY.

...SYNOPSIS...
A MID-LEVEL CUT-OFF LOW WILL TRAVERSE THE DESERT SOUTHWEST AND
IMPINGE ON THE SOUTHERN HIGH PLAINS ON SATURDAY. THIS WILL PROVOKE A
LOW-LEVEL MASS RESPONSE IN THE FORM OF NORTHWESTWARD MOISTURE
ADVECTION THROUGH THE DAY. COOLER TEMPERATURES AND STRONGER FLOW
ALOFT ASSOCIATED WITH THE CUT-OFF LOW WILL OVERSPREAD THE LOW-LEVEL
MOISTURE, RESULTING IN ENOUGH BUOYANCY AND SHEAR OVERLAP TO SUPPORT
AN ISOLATED SEVERE THREAT.

...PORTIONS OF CENTRAL NM INTO FAR WEST TX...
THROUGH THE AFTERNOON, 40-50 KT SOUTH-SOUTHWESTERLY MID-LEVEL FLOW
AND 7+ C/KM MID-LEVEL LAPSE RATES FROM THE APPROACHING CUT-OFF LOW
WILL OVERSPREAD MID TO UPPER 50S F SURFACE DEWPOINTS AND BACKED
SOUTHEASTERLY SURFACE WINDS. WITH SURFACE HEATING OF THE
AFOREMENTIONED MOIST LOW-LEVEL AIRMASS, MLCAPE MAY EXCEED 1000 J/KG,
PERHAPS LOCALLY EXCEEDING 1500 J/KG IN SPOTS GIVEN LOCALLY GREATER
INSOLATION. VEERING AND STRENGTHENING FLOW WITH HEIGHT WILL YIELD
MODESTLY CURVED AND ELONGATED HODOGRAPHS, WITH OVER 40 KTS OF
EFFECTIVE BULK SHEAR EXPECTED. MULTICELLS AND PERHAPS A COUPLE OF
SUPERCELLS MAY DEVELOP AND PERSIST FOR AT LEAST A FEW HOURS BEFORE
MERGING INTO A LOOSELY ORGANIZED LINE OF THUNDERSTORMS. STORMS
SHOULD BE AT THEIR STRONGEST DURING THEIR SUPERCELL STAGE, CAPABLE
OF PRODUCING ISOLATED SEVERE HAIL/WIND AND PERHAPS A TORNADO.
