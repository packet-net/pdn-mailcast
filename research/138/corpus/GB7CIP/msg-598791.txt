==================================================
1. EVE 2026 EXPERIMENT OVERVIEW
==================================================
ON OCTOBER 17-18, 2026, THE DWINGELOO RADIO TELESCOPE
TRANSMITTED SIGNALS TOWARDS VENUS, WHICH WERE REFLECTED
AND RECEIVED BY ASTROPEILER STOCKERT. SIGNALS TRAVELED
ABOUT 42 MILLION KM EACH WAY (~5 MINUTES ROUND TRIP).
FROM THE ECHOES, THE TEAM SUCCESSFULLY DECODED A
10-CHARACTER MESSAGE: "VENUS ECHO".

==================================================
2. TECHNOLOGY & COLLABORATION
==================================================
THIS EVE EXPERIMENT BUILDS ON EARTH-MOON-EARTH (EME)
PRACTICES. PREVIOUS SUCCESSES BY AMATEURS OCCURRED
ONLY IN 2009 (AMSAT-DL) AND 2025.
- **M-FSK WAVEFORM**: DEVELOPED IN COLLABORATION WITH
  THE OPEN RESEARCH INSTITUTE (ORI), REQUIRING 40
  MINUTES OF SIGNAL AVERAGING TO DECODE.
- **PROFESSIONAL TELESCOPES**: WESTERBORK (WSRT), 100M
  EFFELSBERG, 76M LOVELL, AND eMERLIN NETWORK DISHES
  ALSO MONITORED THE REFLECTIONS.
