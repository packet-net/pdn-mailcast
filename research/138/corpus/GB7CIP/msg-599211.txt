DX Activities
=============

6W, SENEGAL:
     An Italian team (Elvira IV3FSG, Franco I1FQH, Alfeo I1HJT, Tony
     I2PJA, Silvano I2YSB, Oscar IK2AQZ, Angelo IK2CKR, and Stefano
     IK2HKT) will be active between Oc.t 28 and Nov. 9 as 6W/I2YSB (CW,
     SSB, RTTY) and 6W/IK2HKT (FT8) on 160-6m (including 60m) with 5
     rigs. QSL direct via I2YSB, OQRS.

9X, RUANDA:
     Al, F8FUA, operates holiday-style as 9X5KM from Kigali between Oct.
     31 and Nov. 11. QRV on CW, SSB, digital modes with a focus on the
     lower bands. QSL via F8FUA, LoTW.

ON, BELGIUM:
     OR80AF marks the 80th anniverary of the Belgian Air Force.

PY, BRAZIL:
     The Araucaria DX Group celebrates its 50th anniversary with the
     callsign ZW50B until the end of October.

PY, BRAZIL:
     ZZ5UNO operates around the Brazilian Amateur Radio Day 2026 (Nov.
     5) between Oct. 31 and Nov. 15.

ZA, ALBANIA:
     ZA/AE5X remains active until the end of October. QRV on 20-10m (CW)
     with 10 watts; possibly from POTA locations. QSL via LoTW.

     and a multiband vertical antenna. QSL via M0OXO.
