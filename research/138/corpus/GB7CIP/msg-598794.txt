WATCHES AND WARNINGS
--------------------
There are no coastal watches or warnings in effect.

Interests along the southwestern coast of Mexico should monitor the
progress of Vance. A Tropical Storm Watch may be required for
portions of the southwestern coast of Mexico later today or tonight.


DISCUSSION AND OUTLOOK
----------------------
At 900 AM CST (1500 UTC), the center of Hurricane Vance was located
near latitude 12.9 North, longitude 103.0 West. Vance is moving
toward the north-northwest near 6 mph (9 km/h), and a slow motion
toward the north-northwest is expected to continue through the
weekend.

Maximum sustained winds are now near 105 mph (165 km/h) with higher
gusts. Gradual weakening is expected through Sunday. Some
re-intensification will be possible beginning Sunday night or
Monday.

Hurricane-force winds extend outward up to 30 miles (45 km) from the
center and tropical-storm-force winds extend outward up to 150 miles
(240 km).

The estimated minimum central pressure is 970 mb (28.65 inches).
