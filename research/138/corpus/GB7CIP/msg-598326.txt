1. OVERVIEW AND BACKGROUND OF THE VICTORIAN EME FEAT (1966)
======================================================================
DETAILED RETROSPECTIVE OF AUSTRALIA'S FIRST SUCCESSFUL EARTH-MOON-EARTH (EME)
COMMUNICATION PERFORMED BY AMATEUR RADIO OPERATOR RAY NAUGHTON, AN ELECTRONICS
STORE OWNER FROM BIRCHIP, WESTERN VICTORIA.
DESPITE U.S. SCIENTISTS INSISTING THAT A FULL KILOWATT AND EXPENSIVE COMMERCIAL
GEAR WERE REQUIRED, NAUGHTON ACHIEVED THE LUNAR BOUNCE USING JUST **100 WATTS**âONE-TENTH
OF THE RECOMMENDED POWERâBY STRINGING LONG WIRE RHOMBIC ANTENNAS ACROSS TIMBER TOWERS
AND TELEPHONE POLES SPANNING 820 FEET WIDE (WIDER THAN TWO FOOTBALL FIELDS) AND
ROUGHLY 100 FEET HIGH.

======================================================================
2. MILESTONES, CONTACT CHRONOLOGY, AND RECOGNITION
======================================================================
- **JULY 1966**: FIRST COPYABLE SIGNALS RECEIVED BY A CALIFORNIA STATION AFTER MONTHS
  OF PERSISTENT TESTING.
- **NOVEMBER 1966**: COMPLETED TWO-WAY CONTACT WITH A NEW JERSEY OPERATOR.
- **DECEMBER 1966**: FULL TWO-WAY CONTACT ESTABLISHED WITH CALIFORNIA AFTER NINE
  MONTHS OF TRYING.
HIS SUCCESS DISPROVED EXPERTS WHO DEEMED HIS RIG "LIMITED," EARNING HIM A FULLY FUNDED
TRIP TO THE UNITED STATES TO SHARE HIS HOMEMADE ANTENNA DESIGN METHODOLOGY WITH RESEARCHERS.

======================================================================
3. PHYSICAL PRINCIPLES & MODERN EME EVOLUTION
======================================================================
- **LUNAR REFLECTION PHYSICS**: THE MOON ACTS AS A PASSIVE REFLECTOR ROUGHLY 240,000
  MILES AWAY, SCATTERING RADIO WAVES ACROSS ROCKY TERRAIN WITH ONLY A TINY FRACTION
  OF ENERGY BOUNCING BACK TO EARTH.
- **LOW-POWER ADVANCEMENTS**: DECADES LATER, OPERATORS IN TASMANIA PUSHED THE LIMITS
  USING AN 85-FOOT DISH TO REFLECT A DIGITAL DATA SIGNAL TO A DUTCH OBSERVATORY AT JUST
  **3 MILLIWATTS**, AIDED BY ADVANCED WEAK-SIGNAL DIGITAL MODES FAR BELOW MORSE THRESHOLDS.
