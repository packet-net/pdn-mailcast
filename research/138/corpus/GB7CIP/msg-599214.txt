A Nor'easter situated well-off the Mid-Atlantic coastline is
forecast to track north and eastward over the next couple of days.
In the meantime, persistent and strong onshore winds will result
in coastal flooding, dangerous marine conditions and beach
erosion, especially at high tide cycles, from southern New Jersey
through the North Carolina Coast. Wind gusts in excess of 40 mph
are possible, especially across the Outer Banks region of North
Carolina. Conditions should begin to improve on Thursday.

Elsewhere, an amplifying upper trough in the East Pacific will tap
tropical moisture as it digs into the Western U.S.. This anomalous
moisture will surge northward into the Southwest and Four Corners
region starting today. Scattered to isolated showers and
thunderstorms will spread across the Four Corners while, an
approaching surface low pressure system focuses additional storms
across Central/Southern California. Slight risks (at least 15chance) of excessive rainfall leading to flash flooding are in
