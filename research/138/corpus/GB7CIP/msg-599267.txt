Tropical Weather Discussion for North America, Central America
Gulf of America, Caribbean Sea, northern sections of South
America, and Atlantic Ocean to the African coast from the

...SPECIAL FEATURES...

Gales and Significant Swell N of Bahamas: A tight pressure
gradient between a 1032 mb high pressure system over New England
and a stationary front extending from S of Bermuda to a 1010 mb
low just N of central Cuba is supporting fresh to near gale-force
NE winds and rough to very rough seas N of the front and W of the
low. Winds are reaching minimal gale force near 31N73W. Scattered
showers and isolated thunderstorms are noted N of the front
between 65W and 75W. Winds will start to diminish tonight, with
strong NE to E winds and rough to very rough seas persisting N of
28N and W of 65W through Thu night as the low pressure moves
southwestward and the front weakens. Mariners are urged to remain
alert to these dangerous marine conditions and take the necessary
precautions to avoid these affected areas.

Please, see the latest NWS High Seas Forecast issued by the

...MONSOON TROUGH/ITCZ...

The monsoon trough reaches the Atlantic through the coast of
Africa near 12N16W and continues to 09N23W. The ITCZ extends from
09N23W to 09N60W. Scattered moderate to isolated strong
convection is noted from 05N to 11N and between 29W and 59W.

...GULF OF AMERICA...

Moderate to fresh winds, and moderate seas prevail E of 90W. W of
90W, gentle to moderate winds and moderate seas are noted.

For the forecast, a tight pressure gradient between high pressure
system over New England and a complex weather system over the Cay
Sal area of the Bahamas will continue to support fresh to strong
NE to E winds and rough seas in the E Gulf into late this week.
Winds and seas will gradually diminish into Sat as the ridge
slides eastward. Looking ahead, another robust high pressure
system will reach New England late this weekend and early next
week allowing winds and seas to increase in the NE Gulf.
Elsewhere, gentle to moderate winds and moderate seas will prevail
into early next week. Expect fresh SE to S winds over much of the
Gulf by Mon between high pressure over the western Atlantic and a
cold front moving through the Southern Plains.

...CARIBBEAN SEA...

A weak pressure gradient is supporting gentle to moderate winds
and slight to moderate seas.

For the forecast, a surface trough extends along 80W north of 15N
into central Cuba. The trough will drift westward and weaken over
the next couple of days. Ample tropical moisture and upper- level
divergence support scattered showers and isolated thunderstorms
over the central Caribbean. Otherwise, moderate to locally fresh
trade winds in the E Caribbean will continue over the next few
days, gradually extending westward into the south- central
Caribbean through the week and into the upcoming weekend.

...ATLANTIC OCEAN...

Please see the Special Features section above for information on
a gale warning and significant swell causing very rough seas
north of the Bahamas.

Aside from the conditions mentioned in the Special Features
section above, winds are moderate or weaker, with moderate seas.

For the forecast west of 55W, a tight pressure gradient between
high pressure system over New England and a stationary front
extending from SE of Bermuda to a 1010 mb low in the Cay Sal area
of the Bahamas supports strong to gale-force NE winds and rough to
very rough seas north of the front. Scattered showers and
isolated thunderstorms are occurring east of the Bahamas. Strong
NE to E winds and rough to very rough seas will persist N of 28N
and W of 65W through Thu night as the low pressure moves
southwestward and the front weakens. Weak low pressure will
develop southwest of Bermuda by Fri, then lift northeastward and
reinforce the frontal boundary over the waters between the central
Bahamas and Bermuda. Expect fresh to strong NE-E winds and seas
to 11 ft N of the front through early next week.
