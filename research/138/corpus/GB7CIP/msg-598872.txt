Title: RESEARCHERS SUCCESSFULLY DEMONSTRATE PLASMA BEAM ANTENNA

SCIENTISTS DEMONSTRATE LASER-INDUCED-PLASMA-FILAMENT (LIPF) ANTENNA TRANSMITTING VHF

AUTHORS: PRYA DARSHNI & PAUL FRANZON (NC STATE) / ARTHUR DOGARIU
DATE: OCTOBER 25, 2026
ISSUED VIA BPQ32 NODE HP2DFA-5 IN PANAMA

==================================================
1. INNOVATION OVERVIEW
==================================================
RESEARCHERS AT NORTH CAROLINA STATE UNIVERSITY HAVE
SUCCESSFULLY DEMONSTRATED A TECHNIQUE USING A LASER
TO PRODUCE A TUNABLE PLASMA BEAM ANTENNA CAPABLE
OF TRANSMITTING RADIO WAVES. DESCRIBED AS LOOKING
LIKE A "LIGHTSABER," THIS SYSTEM ALLOWS FOR FREQUENCY
SWEEPING AND CUSTOMIZED ANTENNA LENGTHS WITHOUT
COMPLICATED MECHANICAL DEPLOYMENT MECHANISMS.

==================================================
2. OPERATION & CONTACTLESS FEED
==================================================
BY FIRING A LASER BEAM, RESEARCHERS IONIZE A THIN
SHAFT OF AIR INTO A PLASMA FILAMENT. TO SOLVE THE
CHALLENGE OF CONNECTING RADIO TECHNOLOGY WITHOUT
DISTORTING THE PLASMA, THEY DESIGNED A CAPACITIVELY-
COUPLED CONTACTLESS FEED USING A METAL RING. FEEDING
A 30 MHZ RF SIGNAL INTO THE CAPACITOR INDUCED THE
PLASMA FILAMENT TO RADIATE ITS OWN ELECTROMAGNETIC
FIELD, YIELDING A 150¢NCREASE IN RECEIVED SIGNAL.

==================================================
3. APPLICATIONS & SPACE EXPLORATION
==================================================
THE TECHNIque ENABLES BEAM STEERING, CONTROLLING
THE ANTENNA'S ANGLE AND LENGTH BY SHIFTING THE LASER.
LONG-TERM PROSPECTS INCLUDE SATELLITES AND LEO
SPACE EXPLORATION WHERE PAYLOAD CONSTRAINTS AND WIDE-
BAND SCANNING ARE CRITICAL. THE STUDY WAS PUBLISHED
IN THE *IEEE JOURNAL OF MICROWAVES* (DOI: 10.1109/JMW.2026.3722433).
