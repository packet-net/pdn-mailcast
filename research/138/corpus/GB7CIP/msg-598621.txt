Warning

Mercury v1.9.20 does not connect to Mercury 1.9.19 or any older version
over the air, in either direction. The connect frames have a new packet
type, and the versions refuse each other cleanly: nothing stays keyed.
Upgrade every station you talk to at once. Read RELEASE-1.9.20.md
before upgrading.

---

All stations are urged to upgrade immediately to insure compatibility with those 
running v1.9.20.

for a  current list of my frequencies and services offered. Stations within range are 
welcome to test Mercury Modem with my system.

https://github.com/Rhizomatica/mercury/releases/

I look forward to observing stations testing Mercury Modem v1.9.20. Thanks for reading.
