======================================================================
1. 50TH ANNIVERSARY IN LIMERICK, MONTCO
======================================================================
THE **POTTSTOWN AREA AMATEUR RADIO CLUB (PAARC)**, LOCATED IN LIMERICK, PENNSYLVANIA
(MONTGOMERY COUNTY), IS CELEBRATING ITS **50TH ANNIVERSARY IN 2026**. INITIALLY
STARTED AROUND 1970 BY ED (K3BVQ) AND RAY (W3ZVY) at Pottstown Senior High School
to revive a dormant local club, the organization grew into the Pottstown Area Repeater
Team before officially becoming PAARC in 2000. The club's call sign, **K3ZMC**,
honors the late Al Dolenti.

======================================================================
2. ON-AIR NETWORKS & TECHNICAL ACTIVITIES
======================================================================
PAARC MAINTAINS A CONSISTENT CALENDAR OF EDUCATIONAL TALKS AND ON-AIR NETS:
- **10-METER NETS**: HELD ON TUESDAYS AND THURSDAYS, ANCHORED BY CO-FOUNDER ED (K3BVQ).
- **WEEKLY REPEATER NETS (WEDNESDAYS & SUNDAYS)**:
  * **WEDNESDAY (2-METER)**: AT 7:30 PM ON **147.210 MHZ**, featuring trivia questions.
  * **SUNDAY (UHF)**: AT 7:30 PM ON **443.55 MHZ**.
- **2026 TECHNICAL TOPICS**: RECENT MEETINGS AND DISCUSSIONS HAVE EXPLORED OFF-GRID
  MESSAGING PLATFORMS LIKE MESHTASTIC AND MESHCORE, RADIO ASTRONOMY, SPACE WEATHER,
  GNU RADIO SOFTWARE, AND PARTICIPATION IN THE PENNSYLVANIA QSO PARTY.

======================================================================
3. LICENSING EXAMS & MONTHLY MEETINGS
======================================================================
- **LICENSE TESTING**: PAARC HOSTS EXAM SESSIONS FOR THE ENTRY-LEVEL TECHNICIAN LICENSE
  ON MOST FIRST FRIDAYS AT 5:00 PM (EXCEPT JULY AND AUGUST). THE NEXT EXAM DATE IS NOVEMBER 6.
- **MONTHLY MEETINGS**: OPEN TO ALL, HELD ON THE FIRST FRIDAY OF EACH MONTH AT 7:00 PM
  AT ST. JAMES LUTHERAN CHURCH (93 KUGLER ROAD, LIMERICK). ARRL EASTERN PENNSYLVANIA
  SECTION MANAGER MATT BUTCH IS SCHEDULED TO SPEAK ON NOVEMBER 6.
