DISCUSSION AND OUTLOOK
----------------------
At 800 PM MST (0300 UTC), the center of Post-Tropical Cyclone Trudy
was located near latitude 19.5 North, longitude 108.0 West. The
post-tropical cyclone is drifting northward, but is expected to move
slowly to the west-northwest over the next day or so until
dissipation.

Maximum sustained winds have decreased to near 35 mph (55 km/h) with
higher gusts.

The estimated minimum central pressure is 1005 mb (29.68 inches).
