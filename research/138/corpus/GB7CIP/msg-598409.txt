======================================================================
1. TRANSPORTER-18 MISSION OVERVIEW
======================================================================
RIDESHARE LAUNCHES HAVE BECOME ONE OF THE MOST EFFECTIVE WAYS FOR THE AMATEUR
SATELLITE COMMUNITY TO DEPLOY HARDWARE INTO ORBIT. SPACEX'S **TRANSPORTER-18**
RIDESHARE MISSION, LAUNCHED ON OCTOBER 1, 2026, FROM VANDENBERG SPACE FORCE BASE
IN CALIFORNIA, RECENTLY DELIVERED ONE OF THE STRONGEST SHOWINGS YET WITH **SIX
SATELLITES FROM THREE DIFFERENT AMSAT ORGANIZATIONS** REACHING ORBIT SUCCESSFULLY.

======================================================================
2. INTERNATIONAL SATELLITE LINEUP & DEPLOYMENT
======================================================================
THE SPACECRAFT RODE ABOARD **OSSIE-D**, THE FIRST ORBITAL TRANSFER VEHICLE
DEVELOPED BY SPANISH SPACE TRANSPORTATION COMPANY UARX SPACE, WHICH BEGAN
DEPLOYING ITS SMALL SATELLITE PAYLOAD AFTER SEPARATING FROM THE FALCON 9 UPPER STAGE:

- **AMSAT-DL (GERMANY)**:
  * PAYLOAD: THREE (3) POCKETQUBES NAMED **ERMINAZ-2**.
- **AMSAT-EA (SPAIN)**:
  * PAYLOAD: TWO (2) SATELLITES DESIGNATED **HADES-L** AND **UNNE-1B**.
- **AMSAT NEPAL (NEPAL)**:
  * PAYLOAD: ONE (1) SATELLITE NAMED **SANOSAT-2**.

======================================================================
3. TECHNICAL IMPACT & COMMUNITY OUTLOOK
======================================================================
THIS MULTI-NATION LAUNCH REPRESENTS A MAJOR MILESTONE IN GLOBAL AMSAT
COLLABORATION, EXPANDING CAPABILITIES FOR AMATEUR RADIO EXPERIMENTATION,
TELEMETRY RECEPTION, AND EDUCATIONAL SPACE NETWORKS UTILIZING ADVANCED ORBITAL
TRANSFER VEHICLES.
