Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity continued at low levels. There are two numbered sunspot
regions on the visible disk: Region 4557 (N07W68, Dao/beta) showed
modest new flux emergence in its intermediate spots following a period
of decay, producing a C1.4/Sf flare at 22/1733 UTC. Region 4560 (N13W42,
Eai/beta-gamma) was the primary driver of flaring, producing the largest
event of the period-a C3.5 flare at 22/0309 UTC. The region continued to
undergo flux emergence in its intermediate portions and briefly
developed a delta magnetic configuration alongside its mixed gamma
polarities.

No new Earth-directed CMEs were observed in available coronagraph
imagery.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is expected to remain at low levels through 25 Oct, with
a slight chance for isolated M-class flares (R1-R2, Minor-Moderate),
primarily due to the flare potential and ongoing development of Region
4560.

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux was at moderate to high levels,
briefly exceeding the 1,000 pfu threshold for a single reading before
returning below high levels. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux
remained steady at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to reach high levels on
23-25 Oct in response to coronal hole high-speed stream (CH HSS)
influences. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is anticipated to
persist at background levels through 25 Oct.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters reflected diminishing positive polarity CH HSS
influences. Total magnetic field strength (Bt) steadily declined over
the period, moving from 9 nT down to 5 nT. The North-South (Bz)
component exhibited multiple extended southward deflections, reaching
peak negative values between -5 nT and -7 nT. Solar wind speeds
gradually drifted downward from early values around 480-490 km/s to
around 430-440 km/s late in the period. The phi angle remained
predominantly positive (away from the Sun).

.Forecast...
Positive polarity CH HSS influences are anticipated to continue waning
through 23 Oct, returning to mostly background-like conditions by early
24 Oct. Enhancements are likely to return on 25 Oct due to the combined
influences of a negative polarity CH HSS and possible glancing CME
effects from the 21 Oct eruptions.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic field reached mostly active levels due to ongoing
positive polarity CH HSS influences.

.Forecast...
Quiet to unsettled conditions are expected on 23 Oct as CH HSS
influences wane, transitioning to mostly quiet levels on 24 Oct. A
return to active levels, with a chance for geomagnetic storming, is
expected on 25 Oct due to low-confidence combined effects from a
recently emerged negative polarity coronal hole and potential glancing
CME impacts.
