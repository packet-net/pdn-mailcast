WATCHES AND WARNINGS
--------------------
There are no coastal watches or warnings in effect.

Interests along the southwestern coast of Mexico should monitor the
progress of Vance.


DISCUSSION AND OUTLOOK
----------------------
At 300 PM CST (2100 UTC), the center of Hurricane Vance was located
near latitude 11.2 North, longitude 102.3 West. Vance is moving
toward the northwest near 5 mph (7 km/h). A slow motion toward the
north-northwest or northwest is expected to continue through the
weekend.

Maximum sustained winds have increased to near 105 mph (165 km/h)
with higher gusts.  Steady to rapid strengthening is expected
through tonight, and Vance is forecast to become a major hurricane.
After that, slow weakening is forecast to start on Saturday night
and continue through Sunday.

Hurricane-force winds extend outward up to 35 miles (55 km) from the
center and tropical-storm-force winds extend outward up to 205 miles
(335 km).

The estimated minimum central pressure is 971 mb (28.68 inches).


HAZARDS AFFECTING LAND
----------------------
SURF: Swells generated by Vance are expected to begin affecting
portions of the coast of southern and southwestern Mexico this
weekend and continue into next week. These swells are likely to
cause life-threatening surf and rip current conditions. Please
consult products from your local weather office.
