An upper-level low over Southern California and the Southwest will
weaken and move northeastward to the Northern Plains by Tuesday.
Moisture from the Gulf will stream northwestward into the
Southwest/Southern High Plains, producing showers and
thunderstorms with heavy rain. Therefore, the WPC has issued a
Slight Risk (level 2/4) of excessive rainfall with these
thunderstorms over parts of the Central/Southern High Plains and
Central/Southern Rockies on Sunday through Monday morning. The
associated heavy rain will create mainly localized areas of flash
flooding, with urban areas, roads, small streams, narrow
canyons/gullies, and burn scars the most vulnerable.

Also on Sunday, easterly flow off the Atlantic will produce
showers and thunderstorms with heavy rain over the eastern coast
of Florida. Therefore, the WPC has issued a Slight Risk (level
2/4) of excessive rainfall from these thunderstorms over parts of
the eastern coast of Florida on Sunday through Monday morning. The
associated heavy rain will create mainly localized areas of flash
flooding, with urban areas, roads, small streams, and low-lying
areas being the most vulnerable. In addition, the easterly flow
off the Atlantic will continue to produce showers and
thunderstorms over parts of the Southeast Coast and the east coast
of Florida through Tuesday.

Moreover, a front extending from the Upper Great Lakes
southwestward to the Southern High Plains will move eastward to
off the Northeast Coast and extend westward to the Northern
Rockies by Tuesday. Rain will develop over parts of the Upper
Great Lakes into the Central Plains on Sunday.

Overnight Sunday, a low-pressure wave over the Central High Plains
will move northeastward into the Northern Plains/Upper Mississippi
Valley by Tuesday. Rain will develop over the Northern/Central
Plains Sunday night and move into the Northern Plains/Upper
Mississippi Valley through Tuesday morning. Snow will develop over
parts of the Northern Plains late Sunday night into late Monday
night. Showers and thunderstorms will also move out of the
Central/Southern High Plains into the Plains by Tuesday.

In addition, a front will move onshore over the Pacific Northwest
and inland to the Northern High Plains by Tuesday. Rain will
develop over the Pacific Northwest and move inland to the Northern
Intermountain Region with snow at higher elevations Monday into
Tuesday.
