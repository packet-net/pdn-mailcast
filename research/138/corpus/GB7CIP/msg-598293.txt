Trying to beam up on weak fast scan ATV signals with tight beams on a TV mon is
not that easy. Here is a way that greatly helps when you don't quiet know the
beam heading (e.g. /P). It is of no use for digital TV modes!

Take the recovered video and feed it to the TV audio stages (decent size LS or
HiFi headphones!) after some CR filtering to remove the line timebase & treble
noise.

This works by listening to the 50Hz buzz, as your ear/brain is a few dB better 
than the TV sync stages. It also works on 10GHz/1255MHz FM TV just as well as
AM mode.

The extra 6k8 load on the 75ohm video line has no effect.
