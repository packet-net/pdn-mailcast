2. REPAIR & REWINDING SOLUTIONS
==================================================
JOHN (M5ET) NOTED THE OLD G5400 IS LARGELY IDENTICAL
TO KENPRO'S KR5400 (PAIRING A KR500 ELEVATION AND
KR400 AZIMUTH ROTOR).
- **MANUAL REWINDING**: IF A MOTOR WINDING IS BAD,
  IT CAN LIKELY BE REWOUND BY HAND TO SALVAGE THE UNIT.
- **USEFUL REFERENCES**:
  * W5IM KR500RW Guide: https://www.qsl.net/w5im/KR500RW.html
