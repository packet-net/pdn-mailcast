Solar Activity

.24 hr Summary...
Solar activity was at very low levels in the past 24h, with few B-class
flares from the only numbered Region 4560 (N13W77, Fao/beta-gamma).
Region 4560 presented some decay in its intermediary spots as continued
to approach the West limb. Two spotless plage regions were observed but
not yet numbered during the period: one in the NE quadrant and the other
in the SW.

Eruptions were observed beyond the NE limb in SUVI imagery and
associated with two CMEs first seen in LASCO/C2 imagery around 25/0312
UTC and 25/0636 UTC. No Earth-directed component is expected from these
events. No new Earth-directed CMEs were observed in available
coronagraph imagery.

.Forecast...
Solar activity is likely to remain at very-low to low levels through 27
Oct, with a slight chance for isolated M-class flares (R1-R2,
Minor-Moderate).

Energetic Particle

.24 hr Summary...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux reached high levels, with a peak
flux of 3,259 pfu observed at 24/1530 UTC. The greater than 10 MeV
proton flux remained steady at background levels.

.Forecast...
The greater than 2 MeV electron flux is expected to continue reaching
high levels through 27 Oct. The greater than 10 MeV proton flux is
anticipated to persist at background levels through 27 Oct.

Solar Wind

.24 hr Summary...
Solar wind parameters continued reflecting undisturbed conditions near
Earth. Total magnetic field strength (Bt) remained below 5 nT, with no
significant periods of southward Bz observed. Solar wind speeds declined
to about 350 km/s early 25 Oct and slightly oscillated around this level
until the end of the reported period. Phi angle became variable after
24/1240 UTC, with a sustained ~ 3h period of negative sector (oriented
toward the Sun) around 25/1600-1900 UTC, followed by a predominant
positive sector (away from the Sun) period.

.Forecast...
Enhanced solar wind conditions are likely on 25-26 Oct due to combined
influences of a small, negative polarity CH HSS and possible glancing
CME effects from the 21 Oct eruptions. Nominal conditions are expected
to return on 27 Oct.

Geospace

.24 hr Summary...
The geomagnetic activity was at quiet levels during the period.

.Forecast...
Periods of active conditions, with a chance for G1 (Minor) storming, are
possible on 25 Oct due to combined effects from a negative polarity
coronal hole and potential glancing CME impacts. Quiet to unsettled
conditions are likely on 26-27 Oct.
