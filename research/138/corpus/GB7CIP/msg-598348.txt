DISCUSSION AND OUTLOOK
----------------------
At 200 PM MST (2100 UTC), the center of Tropical Storm Trudy was
located near latitude 19.3 North, longitude 107.9 West. Trudy is
nearly stationary, but is forecast to begin moving to the northwest
over the next 24 hours.

Maximum sustained winds have decreased to 40 mph (65 km/h) with
higher gusts. Further weakening is forecast during the next 48
hours, and Trudy is forecast to become post-tropical by tomorrow.

Tropical-storm-force winds extend outward up to 45 miles (75 km)
from the center.

The estimated minimum central pressure is 1004 mb (29.65 inches).
