1. PLATFORM OVERVIEW

ANNOUNCING THE LAUNCH OF **IONOFLUX** (HTTPS://IONOFLUX.RUNVIA.APP), A NEW FREE

2. KEY FEATURES
======================================================================
- **HF PROPAGATION PREDICTION**: ANALYZE POTENTIAL PATHS BETWEEN TWO LOCATIONS
  USING CURRENT IONOSPHERIC CONDITIONS, FREQUENCY, MODE, POWER, AND ANTENNAS.

  FREQUENCY (MUF) AND TOTAL ELECTRON CONTENT (TEC).
- **SPACE WEATHER MONITORING**: SOLAR FLUX, SUNSPOT NUMBERS, KP/AP INDICES,
  X-RAY ACTIVITY, SOLAR WIND, AND NOAA ALERTS.
- **REAL-WORLD RECEPTION DATA**: EXPLORE AMATEUR RECEPTION OBSERVATIONS FROM
  PSK REPORTER AND WSPR NETWORKS.
- **PROPAGATION FORECASTS**: EXAMINE ESTIMATED HF BAND CONDITIONS UP TO 72 HOURS.
- **FREE API & WIDGETS**: REST API AVAILABLE FOR DEVELOPERS (UP TO 1,000 FREE
  REQUESTS PER UTC DAY) AND WEBSITE EMBEDDING WIDGETS.
