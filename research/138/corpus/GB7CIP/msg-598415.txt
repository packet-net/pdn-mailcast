- 5055kHz is ALIVE!
Al/VK4ALT reports that the newly revamped Radio 4DX 5055kHz Amplitude Modulation 
service is back on-air in test mode at the moment from the transmission site 
at South Johnston west of Innisfail Far North Queensland. 
Al advises that guaranteed hours are daily 4-7pm and overnight on Fri/Sat 
till about 10am. Transmissions usually start on before 4pm and often on after 7pm.
Transmission hours will be revised once the 1kW transmitter is back in service, 
hopefully soon. Presently on about 85 watts AM.
Programme is mostly music aimed at the over (harking back to the 2CH Good Music 
format), with strictly no politics and no smut.
For the SWLs the station now issues QSL cards.

- Clairview Gathering 2027
The Clairview Gathering is a yearly event where Radio Amateurs meet
at a spot on the coast between Rockhampton and Mackay to relax, talk
radio, re-establish acquaintances and participate in the world renown
Saturday Night Mega-Auction. There might even be some fox-hunting and
there will definitely be RAFFLES ! 
The Gathering is one of the main revenue raisers amateur radio clubs
in the region

It happens in 2027 from 
Friday arvo 7th to Sunday 9th May, with most of the action happening 
on Saturday 8th May.
Those who are keen to chill out completely in one of the most relaxing
and laid back venues in the cosmos arrive earlier and stay later.

catering numbers for the Saturday Night feed-up.

Some more details of these events -

Whitsunday Region Endurance Riders Association Inc Euri Gold Farm Endurance Rides.
All the rides happen on the verdant slopes of Euri Gold Farm, just out of Merinda
north of Bowen North Queensland.
You are able to camp out early at Ride Base or the checkpoint from Wednesday onwards.
The ride starts up on Friday morning and will run right through Monday afternoon.
Your chance to get out into some lovely countryside, set up camp with a portable station
or three and communicate in a relaxing fashion as the horsies and riders go by, day and night.
Hey Mackay and Bowen horsie operators - you can join in the fun too !

Denison Creek Horse Endurance Rides
Mackay Amateur Radio Association members provide Communications Support and Checkpoint services
for the Horse Endurance Rides that happen at Denison Creek Station near Epsom Central Queensland.
Their volunteer numbers are dwindling and they need a hand to provide a safety net for the events.
The rides happen on the ramparts of Mount Seaview approx 60km southwest of Mackay and is accessed
via the Peak Downs Highway at 21.482444S 148.810030E. Camping available at Ride Base and the 
event is made up of a number of stationary and mobile checkpoints.
Rides start from 1pm Saturday and finish around 11am Sunday. 
Let Mackay Comms Support Co-ordinator Gary/VK4CUZ know if you can help on mobile 0410462892

NQORA Offroad Racing Events - Millchester Motorsport and Recreation Facility Charters Towers
The North Queensland Offroad Racing Association Inc. runs a series of offroad racing
events at the Millchester Motorsport Facility Charters Towers . They need Autosport and 
Radio savvy people to run checkpoints for the events.
Operate at Checkpoints throughout the day with a ringside seat to see up close
the offroad racing action whilst doing useful things with radios.
Camping overnight Wednesday to Monday Night is available at the NQMS Radio Precinct, 
or if you prefer your creature comforts motels and pubs are available to stay in less 
than 10 minutes away from the venue.

Magnetic Island to Townsville Swim.
A great day out on the water playing with radios to the sound of Migaloo
and a hundred plus marathon swimmers thrashing across Cleveland Bay.
Main comms on VHF, with some UHF comms with clubbies and aircraft.
Operating positions at sea and on land with operational endurance 4 to 5 hours.

Kennedy Region Scouts Nighthawk activity.
Your chance to make a real difference and show the youth of today how useful
Amateur Radio can be. Teams tracking and a chance to set up portable at a spot
you would not normally go to. Arrive at Nighthawk HQ at 2pm on Saturday,
then go to your checkpoint for a night of easy operating in the field tracking the progress 
of Nighthawk teams, with some in-flight entertainment as you observe youth members try to impress 
the Scout Leader judges with finesse in their activity.

Bush to Beach Horse Endurance Rides.
The rides are based out of the North Queensland Equestrian Centre at Bluewater Park
approx 30mins northwest of Townsville. Some checkpoints are camp-outs, others
a temporarily activated during the rides with operators glamping at Ride Base. 
Ride tracks vary from rugged river crossings and slightly undulating land through
the properties near Bluewater Park. VHF with high mounted verticals
or Field Yagis needed to get through the topography and the occasional inversion blocking.
There is excellent and ample camping facilities at the Equestrian Centre and on checkpoints.

Townsville City Autosports Club Ingham Forest Rally.
Play with radios out in the field to the sound of high performance machinery.
Operate at Stop and Start control points and at Rally HQ based at the 
Abergowrie-Longpocket QCWA Hall (S18.6657556 E145.96800060). 
Friday arvo and night campout at HQ authorised.
Provide logistic communications support relaying time control and safety reports from and to officials.
Be operational from 6am Saturday Morning at Race HQ and be shown your checkpoints by Clerk of Course deputies.
Mrs Heckler will be organising sandwiches for Saturday Lunch and competition should be completed
by 5-30pm. Comms on VHF and UHF-CB with directinal yagi arrays deployed from checkpoints 
at Start and Stop points both for VHF and UHFCB.

- WICEN VK4 NET
Operators interested in maintaining reliable contact in case of infrastructure
failures in Queensland hold a net every Sunday. 
The net happens on 
14125kHz from 8-00am (2200UTC)
10122kHz from 8-00am (2200UTC)
7105kHz +/- QRM from 8-30am (2230UTC)

The net control station calls in regular stations and then invites 
new stations to call in. 
If conditions are poor other relay stations will attempt to
assist contacts. If conditions are very poor Net Control might move 
the net another frequency (including 3600kHz) depending on how responsive 
the net participants are.
Mix it with other operators, be ready to assist and call in on the net !!
