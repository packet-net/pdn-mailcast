WATCHES AND WARNINGS
--------------------
There are no coastal watches or warnings in effect.

Interests along the southwestern and west-central coasts of Mexico
should monitor the progress of Vance.


DISCUSSION AND OUTLOOK
----------------------
At 800 PM MST (0300 UTC), the center of Tropical Storm Vance was
located near latitude 15.5 North, longitude 106.3 West. Vance is
moving toward the west near 5 mph (7 km/h). A turn to the northwest
is expected on Tuesday, followed by a northward turn on Wednesday.

Maximum sustained winds have decreased to near 50 mph (85 km/h)
with higher gusts. Gradual weakening is forecast during the next few
days.

Tropical-storm-force winds extend outward up to 105 miles (165 km)
from the center.

The estimated minimum central pressure is 999 mb (29.50 inches).


HAZARDS AFFECTING LAND
----------------------
RAINFALL: Through Thursday, Vance is expected to produce 2 to 4
inches of rain, with isolated higher totals of 7 inches possible,
across southern Sinaloa, southwest Durango, and coastal sections of
Nayarit and Jalisco.   This rainfall may produce flooding and
mudslides, especially in areas along steep terrain.

For a complete depiction of forecast rainfall associated with Vance,
please see the National Weather Service Storm Total Rainfall Graphic
available at hurricanes.gov/graphics_ep2.shtml?rainqpf.

SURF: Swells generated by Vance will affect portions of the coast of
southern, southwestern and west-central Mexico during the next few
days. These swells are likely to cause life-threatening surf and rip
current conditions. Please consult products from your local weather
office.
