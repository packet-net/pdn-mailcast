NWS STORM PREDICTION CENTER NORMAN OK
0152 AM CDT MON OCT 19 2026

VALID 211200Z - 221200Z

...THERE IS A MARGINAL RISK OF SEVERE THUNDERSTORMS ACROSS PORTIONS
OF CENTRAL AND EASTERN ARIZONA INTO FAR WESTERN NEW MEXICO...

...SUMMARY...
ISOLATED SEVERE WIND/HAIL AND PERHAPS A BRIEF TORNADO MAY OCCUR OVER
PORTIONS OF ARIZONA INTO WESTERN NEW MEXICO ON WEDNESDAY.

...SYNOPSIS...
AN UPPER TROUGH WILL CONTINUE TO DEEPEN WHILE TRAVERSING THE
INTERIOR WEST ON WEDNESDAY, PROVOKING A STRONG LOW-LEVEL MASS
RESPONSE ACROSS THE DESERT SOUTHWEST AND ROCKIES INTO THE HIGH
PLAINS. ADEQUATE LOW-LEVEL MOISTURE WILL STREAM NORTHWARD ACROSS CA
AND THE INTERIOR WEST, BENEATH STRONG, NEARLY UNIDIRECTIONAL
SOUTHWESTERLY FLOW ALOFT. AT LEAST SCATTERED THUNDERSTORMS ARE
ANTICIPATED ACROSS MUCH OF THE CONUS WEST OF THE ROCKIES. HOWEVER,
THE RICHEST LOW-LEVEL MOISTURE AND ASSOCIATED BUOYANCY WILL RESIDE
IN THE LOWER COLORADO RIVER BASIN, WHERE AN ISOLATED SEVERE THREAT
WILL EXIST.

...LOWER COLORADO RIVER BASIN...
ACROSS THE LOWER COLORADO RIVER BASIN, LOW TO MID 50S F SURFACE
DEWPOINTS WILL ADVECT NORTHWARD BENEATH 6.5+ C/KM MID-LEVEL LAPSE
RATES AND A 50-80 KT 500 MB JET STREAK. 500-1000 J/KG MLCAPE MAY
MANIFEST AMID LARGE AND ELONGATED HODOGRAPHS. THESE CONDITIONS WOULD
SUPPORT MULTICELLS AND PERHAPS A FEW SUPERCELL STRUCTURES WITH AN
ATTENDANT ISOLATED SEVERE WIND/HAIL THREAT AND PERHAPS A BRIEF
TORNADO, WARRANTING THE INTRODUCTION OF CATEGORY 1/MARGINAL RISK
PROBABILITIES THIS OUTLOOK.

...CENTRAL CALIFORNIA VALLEY REGION...
ON WEDNESDAY AFTERNOON, A 70+ KT SOUTHERLY 500 MB JET, AS WELL AS 7+
C/KM MID-LEVEL LAPSE RATES, WILL OVERSPREAD CENTRAL AND SOUTHERN CA
AS THE UPPER TROUGH OVERSPREADS THE SAN JOAQUIN VALLEY. THE STRONG,
NEARLY UNIDIRECTIONAL FLOW WILL YIELD SLIGHTLY CURVED, BUT VERY
ELONGATED HODOGRAPHS. SUCH CONDITIONS WOULD TYPICALLY FAVOR THE
DEVELOPMENT OF LOW-TOPPED SUPERCELLS. HOWEVER, SURFACE
TEMPERATURES/DEWPOINTS IN THE UPPER 50S/MID 40S F MAY LIMIT BUOYANCY
CONSIDERABLY, PRECLUDING SEVERE PROBABILITIES THIS OUTLOOK.

..SQUITIERI.. 10/19/2026

...DISCUSSION...

AND EJECT INTO THE CENTRAL AND NORTHERN PLAINS STATES THIS WEEKEND.
THEREAFTER, A MORE ZONAL, PROGRESSIVE UPPER-AIR PATTERN WILL BECOME

TRAVERSE THIS FLOW INTO EARLY NEXT WEEK. AT THE MOMENT, THE BEST
CHANCE FOR ANY SEVERE POTENTIAL (ALBEIT ISOLATED) WILL BE ACROSS THE
DESERT SOUTHWEST ON DAY 4 (THURSDAY) AND ACROSS THE HIGH PLAINS ON
DAY 5 (FRIDAY). IN THE DAYS 4-5 PERIOD, ALONG THE EASTERN PORTION OF
THE UPPER TROUGH, A BELT OF 50-60 KT MID-LEVEL FLOW WILL OVERSPREAD
THE DESERT SOUTHWEST INTO THE SOUTHERN AND CENTRAL HIGH PLAINS, ATOP
MODEST LOW-LEVEL MOISTURE. MARGINAL INSTABILITY AND STRONG VERTICAL
WIND SHEAR SHOULD OVERLAP ACROSS THE DESERT SOUTHWEST ON THURSDAY
AND OVER THE SOUTHERN TO CENTRAL HIGH PLAINS ON FRIDAY TO SUPPORT
SCATTERED STRONG THUNDERSTORMS, A COUPLE OF WHICH MAY BRIEFLY REACH
SEVERE LIMITS. GIVEN THE ANTICIPATED ISOLATED NATURE OF THE SEVERE
THREAT, AS ALSO SUGGESTED BY ML/AI GUIDANCE, SEVERE PROBABILITIES
WERE NOT INTRODUCED THIS OUTLOOK.
