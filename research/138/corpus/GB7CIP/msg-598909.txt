WATCHES AND WARNINGS
--------------------
There are no coastal watches or warnings in effect.

Interests along the southwestern coast of Mexico should monitor the
progress of Vance.


DISCUSSION AND OUTLOOK
----------------------
At 900 AM CST (1500 UTC), the center of Hurricane Vance was located
near latitude 14.3 North, longitude 103.8 West. Vance is moving
toward the northwest near 5 mph (7 km/h). A northwestward to
west-northwestward motion at a slower forward speed is expected over
the next couple of days, followed by a turn toward the
north-northwest around midweek.

Maximum sustained winds have decreased to near 75 mph (120 km/h)
with higher gusts. Gradual weakening is forecast the next several
days.

Hurricane-force winds extend outward up to 30 miles (45 km) from the
center and tropical-storm-force winds extend outward up to 150 miles
(240 km).

The estimated minimum central pressure is 989 mb (29.21 inches).
