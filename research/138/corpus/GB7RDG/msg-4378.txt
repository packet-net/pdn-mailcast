YASME EXCELLENCE AWARD ---> Established  in  2008  by  the  Yasme Foundation
(https://www.yasme.org/), the Excellence Award  is presented to  individuals
and groups who, through their own service, creativity, effort and dedication
have made a significant contribution to amateur radio.  The contribution may
be in recognition of technical, operating or organizational achievement,  as
all three are necessary for  amateur radio to grow and  prosper.
The latest recipient, announced on 18 October, is the Reverse Beacon Network
team (https://www.reversebeacon.net/): Felipe Ceglia  (PY1NB, founder), Nick
Sinanis  (F5VIH),  Mark Glenn (K7MJG),  Dave Pascoe (KM3T, founder and owner
of LiveATC.NET),  Pete Smith (N4ZR),  Dick Williams (W3OA), and  Chris White
(WX7V).
The award "recognizes the contributions to amateur radio by the team in sup-
port of a global, volunteer-operated network  of automated amateur radio re-
ceiver stations (SDR skimmers) that listen to radio bands  and report who is
transmitting  in near-real time.  The network is  invaluable for propagation
testing, antenna comparisons,  contest operating,  and results adjudication.
The network typically has 250 active nodes around the world measuring a sta-
tion's frequency, signal strength, signal to noise radio,  and sending speed
which is then reported live to a central RBN database".
