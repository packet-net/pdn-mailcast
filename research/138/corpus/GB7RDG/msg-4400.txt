Laplink3 cabling Specification
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~
1)  25 way Parallel port connections
2)  25 way to 25 way serial port connections
3)  9 way to 25 way port connections

The parallel connection is roughly twice as fast as the serial connection.
